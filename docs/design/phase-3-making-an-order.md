# Phase 3 — Making an order (maker), issue #13: design note

**Status: design**, written on 2026-11-16, before the code of phase 3 ([#13](https://github.com/Skyman21m/bull-market/issues/13)), without any code, commit or request to a coordinator. Where the code later differs, the code and its pull requests are right, and this note is updated. It follows the design note of phase 2 (`docs/design/phase-2-taking-an-order.md`, « the phase 2 note » below), whose rules and vocabulary it keeps: everything a maker shares with a taker from status 6 on is the phase 2 code, and this note says only what changes.

Every proposal of this note is a proposal: the decisions taken by the owner that it relies on are named with their date, and everything else is listed in § 7 with its default answer.

## 1. The reference flow, as maker

Reference: RoboSats/robosats at commit ff1998c (`ff1998caed30888b2fb60e15022c29063a60f1ba`, the reference of `AGENTS.md`), as for phase 2. Backend paths are relative to its root (`api/…`), frontend paths to `frontend/src/`. Where the backend's own agent notes disagree with the code, the code wins and the disagreement is noted. The reference was not refreshed for this note (`AGENTS.md`, « Refreshing the RoboSats reference », asks it before each phase starts, which needs the network): that is the first ticket of § 6.

**Status 3 never reaches the maker** (the question of the study): the coordinator stores no status 3, and its read shows 3 only to a robot whose own `TakeOrder` is live (`take_order` is filtered on `taker=request.user`, `api/views.py:248-250`, and the injection asks for it, `:381-386`); the maker's `TakeOrder` cannot exist (`validate_already_maker_or_taker`, `api/logics.py:75-100`, refuses with 1000 any robot already maker of an order at 0 to 3, 6 to 11 or 16, and the take is sent only after that check, `api/views.py:572-584`). While a robot prepares its bond, the order stays at 1 in the database, the maker reads 1, and no field of its read says that a pre-take exists. The maker goes from 1 straight to 6 when a taker's bond locks (`follow_invoices.py:243-251`, `Logics.finalize_contract`, `api/logics.py:1321-1380`). The reference client's « A taker has been found! » (`TradeBox/index.tsx:422-438`, `Prompts/TakerFound.tsx:12-15`) is therefore unreachable against this backend; this reading of the code was not observed on a coordinator. The phase 2 parser already refuses status 3 for anyone but an unnamed pre-taker with its bond (`packages/robosats/lib/src/coordinator/coordinator_order.dart:857-860`).

Loops: as in phase 2, the cleaner runs every 5 s (`api/management/commands/clean_orders.py:114-120`) and the invoice follower too; the publication after the bond's lock is seen within seconds.

### 1.2 Creating: `POST /api/make/`

**The request.** The reference client builds the order from the form (`components/MakerForm/MakerForm.tsx:257-275`, `buildOrderAttributes`) and sends it with `Order.make` (`models/Order.model.ts:196-231`) to the coordinator chosen in the form, with the robot's full header, token hash, PGP keys and Nostr key (`models/Robot.model.ts:74-88`). The body: `type` (« Buy » sends 0, « Sell » 1: the form's `fav.type` is the book's filter, inverted by `fav.type === 0 ? 1 : 0`, `MakerForm.tsx:258`), `currency` (an id), `amount` (`null` for a range), `has_range`, `min_amount`, `max_amount`, `payment_method` (the methods joined by spaces, « not specified » when none), `is_explicit` (false: the field defaults so, `Order.model.ts:73`, and the form never sets it), `premium` (0 when the field is empty), `satoshis` (`null`: the current client offers no order priced in sats, although the coordinator still accepts one), `public_duration` and `escrow_duration` (seconds), `bond_size` (percent), `latitude`, `longitude`, `password` (the SHA-256 hex of the password, `MakerForm.tsx:273`, or `null`) and `description` (or `null`). The coordinator's alias picks the coordinator and is not sent.

**What the coordinator checks**, in this order (`api/views.py:90-216`): an authenticated robot (1036, `:99-100`); the body against `MakeOrderSerializer` (`api/serializers.py:587-627`), which applies the model's validators, or an **empty 400 with no error code** (`api/views.py:102-103`): premium −100 to 999 (`api/models/order.py:91-100`), public duration 597.6 s to 86,400 s (`:109-122`), escrow duration 1,800 s to 36,000 s (`:124-133`), bond size 2 to 15 % (`:135-146`), coordinates within their bounds (`:148-168`), password at most 2,000 characters (`:170-176`), description at most 240 (`:178-184`), payment method at most 70 (`api/serializers.py:592-597`); a book under `MAX_PUBLIC_ORDERS` (1037, 100 in `.env-sample:151`, `api/views.py:105-120`); an amount, or both bounds of a range (1039, 1038, `:149-160`); under a row lock on the robot, **one active order per robot** (409 with 1000 to 1003, `:170-176`, `api/logics.py:75-135`); the size in sats at the coordinator's price at that moment (`t0_satoshis`, `api/views.py:201`): 1004 too big, 1005 too small, and for a range 1006 (minimum above the maximum ÷ 1.5), 1007, 1008, 1009 (maximum above 15 times the minimum) (`api/logics.py:138-177`, sizes 20,000 to 500,000 sats by default, `:31-32`, `.env-sample:155-157`); and the country of a meeting point (1010, `api/logics.py:180-188`). Defaults fill what the body leaves out (`api/views.py:140-147`).

**The answer** is a 201 with `ListOrderSerializer` (`api/views.py:216`, `api/serializers.py:83-107`): `id`, `status` 0, `created_at`, `expires_at` (creation + 300 s), `type`, `currency`, `amount`, `has_range`, `min_amount`, `max_amount`, `payment_method`, `is_explicit`, `premium`, `satoshis`, `maker` and `taker` (the coordinator's **internal user ids**, not hash ids), `escrow_duration`, `bond_size`, `latitude`, `longitude`. It has no `is_maker`, no `public_duration`, no description and **no bond invoice**: the bond is made at the maker's first `GET /api/order/` (`api/views.py:373-379`). `api/AGENTS.md` (« Endpoints ») says the make « returns maker bond invoice »: the code disagrees, and the code wins.

After a 201 the reference client opens the order's page and, in Garage Key mode, publishes a Nostr event of account recovery (`models/Garage.model.ts:547-569`).

### 1.3 Limits, price and fees: `/api/limits/` and `/api/info/`

- `GET /api/limits/`, anonymous: per currency, `code`, `price` (the coordinator's price of a bitcoin), `min_amount` and `max_amount` (`MIN_ORDER_SIZE` and `MAX_ORDER_SIZE` at that price, `api/views.py:1177-1197`).
- `GET /api/info/`: `maker_fee` (`FEE` × `MAKER_FEE_SPLIT`, 0.025 % with the sample) and `taker_fee` (`api/views.py:1042-1045`, `.env-sample:107-110`), the default `bond_size`, `min_order_size` and `max_order_size` (whose code default, 250,000, differs from the limits' 500,000, `api/views.py:1052-1053`), and `devfund` (`:1038-1040`).
- The form's limits: the coordinator's `min_amount` and `max_amount` times (1 + premium), the maximum capped by the coordinator's size limit (`calculateSizeLimit`, `models/Coordinator.model.ts:110-127`, already ported as `CoordinatorSizeLimit`, `features/p2p/lib/src/domain/coordinator_size_limit.dart`), then a safety margin, minimum × 1.03 and maximum × 0.98 (`MakerForm.tsx:96`, `:140-158`). A range must have its maximum at least 1.6 and at most 14.8 times its minimum in the client (`components/MakerForm/AmountRange.tsx:54-55`), 1.5 and 15 at the coordinator.
- « Order current rate »: the limits' `price` × (1 + premium), to 5 significant digits (`MakerForm.tsx:160-169`). « Estimated Bond »: the amount (the maximum of a range) over that rate, in sats, times the bond size, rounded down (`utils/bondCalculator.ts:12-41`, `hooks/useBondEstimate.ts:26-39`).

### 1.4 The maker bond

- **Amount**: `int(last_satoshis × bond_size / 100)`, where `last_satoshis` is the order's sats at the coordinator's price when the maker's first read makes the invoice, the largest amount for a range (`api/logics.py:1266-1268`, `:262-275`). The next reads return the same invoice (`:1259-1263`).
- **Deadline**: the order's own, 300 s after the creation; a read after it expires the order and answers 1022 « Invoice expired. You did not confirm publishing the order in time. Make a new order. » once (`api/logics.py:1254-1256`, `api/errors.py:25`), then the order is at 5. The invoice itself is made for `t_to_expire(0)`, padded by 50 % by LND (`api/lightning/lnd.py:287-289`).
- **CLTV**: (24 h of chat + `escrow_duration` + `public_duration` + `EXP_TAKER_BOND_INVOICE`) × 1.7 ÷ 8 min a block (`api/logics.py:1229-1249`, `.env-sample:161-167`). With the reference client's defaults (86,340 s public, 10,800 s escrow, 200 s): 183,740 s × 1.7 ÷ 480 s ≈ **650 blocks**, about 4.5 days at the real 10 minutes a block; at the maximums (24 h, 10 h): 209,000 s × 1.7 ÷ 480 s ≈ **740 blocks**, about 5.1 days. That is the longest a stuck maker bond can stay locked, almost twice a taker bond's (about 344 blocks, the phase 2 note § 1.2).
- **How long it is held** in a normal life: from the lock to the end of the trade, that is the rest of the public duration, then the escrow step and the chat: about 51 hours with the client's defaults, 58 at the maximums; it is returned at « Confirm received » (`api/logics.py:1721-1726`), at a cancel at 1 or 2, at an expiry at 1 or 2, and settled at once when a dispute opens (`api/logics.py:486-510`).
- **Description**, shown by the paying wallet: « {node alias} - Publishing 'Order {id}: BUY|SELL BTC for {amount} {currency}' - Maker bond - This payment WILL FREEZE IN YOUR WALLET, check on the trading platform if the lock was successful. It will be unlocked (fail) unless you cheat or cancel unilaterally. », or « Payment reference: {uuid} … » for a stealth robot (`api/logics.py:1270-1273`, the order's text `api/models/order.py:303-308`).

### 1.5 Publication

When the maker bond locks, the invoice follower publishes the order (`follow_invoices.py:233-240`, `Logics.publish_order`, `api/logics.py:1205-1227`): status 1, `expires_at` = creation + `public_duration`, and for a range the amount cleared and `last_satoshis` computed again at the current price. The REST book lists the orders at 1 without a password (`api/views.py:776-790`), cached 10 s (`BOOK_CACHE_TTL`, `:85`); the coordinator also publishes a Nostr event of kind 38383 for the order, except for an order with a password or when it has no Nostr key configured (`api/nostr.py:25-33`), and the official client's live book comes from those events (`api/AGENTS.md`, « Redis response caching »). This app reads `/api/book/`.

### 1.6 Pause

`POST /api/order/` with `{"action": "pause"}` (`api/views.py:686-689`, `Logics.pause_unpause_public_order`, `api/logics.py:1758-1783`): 1 → 2 or 2 → 1, refused with 1032 for a robot that is not the maker and 1033 at any other status; the answer is the read (`api/views.py:695`). **The body has no direction: the same request pauses and resumes**, so a request sent twice undoes itself. A pause moves no deadline, expires no pre-take, and a pre-taker's bond that locks while the order is paused is returned to it (`follow_invoices.py:243-261`, which finalizes only a public order). The reference client sends it from the pause button of status 1 and « Unpause Order » of status 2, with no confirmation (`TradeBox/index.tsx:310-313`).

### 1.8 Expiry and renewal

- Untaken, the order expires at creation + `public_duration`, public or paused, with its bond returned and no action needed (`api/logics.py:340-358`; « If the order expires untaken, your bond will return to you (no action needed). », `Prompts/PublicWait.tsx:95`).
- **Renewal is not an action of the backend**: « Renew Order » makes a new order with a new `POST /api/make/` from the expired order's own fields (`TradeBox/index.tsx:146-200`), which the coordinator gives a participant at 5, 13, 14 and 15 for that purpose (`public_duration`, `bond_size`, `api/views.py:490-498`). It is a new order, with a new number and a new bond to pay. The maker sees « Renew Order » at 5 (`Prompts/Expired.tsx:40-97`, with « Password » and « Enter a password again » when the order had one) and « Renew » on the screens of 13, 14 and 15 (`Prompts/Successful.tsx:276-285`, `TradeBox/index.tsx:602-690`).
- In Garage Key mode the renewal uses the current robot only if it is « reusable », that is without an active order and with its last order cancelled or expired (`models/Slot.model.ts:293-309`); otherwise it shows « This robot already has an active order. Navigate to a new account to create another order. » or « This robot was involved in a trade. Please navigate to a new account to create orders. » (`TradeBox/index.tsx:150-157`). So « Renew » after a trade always shows the second text in that mode.
- **Two defects of the reference, read and not tried**: the password typed again at a renewal is sent as typed (`TradeBox/index.tsx:180`), where the form sends its SHA-256 (`MakerForm.tsx:273`) and a taker sends the SHA-256 of what it types (`OrderDetails/TakeButton.tsx:333`), so a renewed protected order would refuse its own password (1045); and « Renew » at 13 to 15 passes no password at all (`TradeBox/index.tsx:620-623`, `:643-646`, `:681-684`), so a protected order renewed after a trade would be public.

### 1.9 The take, as the maker lives it

1. A robot takes: a `TakeOrder` is made, the order stays at 1 (`api/logics.py:205-234`); several robots may pre-take at once, and the first whose bond locks wins, the others' bonds being returned (`follow_invoices.py:243-261`). The maker sees none of it (§ 1.1).
2. A pre-taker that does not lock in time, or cancels, changes nothing for the maker.
3. A taker's bond locks: `finalize_contract` (`api/logics.py:1321-1380`): the taker set, a range's amount set to the amount taken, **`last_satoshis` fixed at the coordinator's price at that moment** (`:1332-1335`), the deadline set to now + `escrow_duration` (`:1338-1340`), status 6. This is where the maker joins the trade that phase 2 built, as the buyer of a buy order or the seller of a sell order (`Logics.is_buyer`, `api/logics.py:236-254`). The trade's sats are fixed by the coordinator alone, at a moment the maker does not choose, possibly 24 hours after the creation.

### 1.10 After the take: maker buyer, maker seller

| | Maker **buyer** (buy order) | Maker **seller** (sell order) |
|---|---|---|
| Locks the escrow | the taker | **the maker**: the trade's sats plus the maker's fee (`Logics.escrow_amount`, `api/logics.py:733-749`) |
| Gives the payout | **the maker**: the trade's sats less the maker's fee (`Logics.payout_amount`, `:650-669`, the maker's fee at `:659-660`) | the taker |
| The maker's step not done in time | at 8, 5 « Invoice not submitted »: **the maker bond settled**, half of it to the taker, the escrow returned (`:423-443`) | at 7, 5 « Escrow not locked »: **the maker bond settled**, half of it to the taker (`:381-402`) |
| The taker's step not done in time | at 7: the taker's bond settled, half of it to the maker, **the order public again** with the maker bond still locked (`:404-421`) | at 8: the taker's bond settled, half of it to the maker, **the escrow returned**, the order public again (`:445-458`) |
| Nobody acted at 6 | both bonds settled, 5 (`:360-379`) | the same |
| The taker cancels at 6 or 7 | its bond settled, half of it to the maker, the order public again (`:1110-1130`) | the same; an escrow invoice made before is only detached (`publish_order`, `:1215-1220`), and a payment that locks it later is returned at once by the invoice follower's « Weird! » branch (`follow_invoices.py:270-280`) |
| The chat runs out | a dispute opens: the escrow and both bonds to the coordinator (`:460-468`, `:486-510`) | the same |
| The maker's fee | 0.025 % with the sample, taken from its payout | 0.025 %, added to its escrow |

**Back in the book** (the taker's default or cancel): the order is public again with **the same deadline as at the start**, creation + public duration (`api/logics.py:1207-1209`); when that deadline has passed, the cleaner expires the order within seconds and returns the maker bond (`clean_orders.py:48-70`, `api/logics.py:340-358`). The order can be taken again; for a maker seller the next take asks **a new escrow invoice**, since `publish_order` cleared the old one (`api/logics.py:1218`) and the invoice is made only when none is attached (`:1491-1495`).

**Rewards**: half of a bond the other side loses is credited to the robot's `earned_rewards` (`Logics.add_slashed_rewards`, `api/logics.py:1822-1880`, `SLASHED_BOND_REWARD_SPLIT`, `.env-sample:222`). When a range order is taken for less than its maximum, a maker that loses its bond loses only as much as the taker's bond, and the rest of its bond is credited back to it as rewards when above 100 sats (`:1836-1841`, `:1869`). Rewards are claimed with an invoice (`POST /api/reward/`, `api/views.py:1077-1108`), which this app does not do yet (phase 4).

## 2. What phase 3 delivers, slice by slice

Principle, as in phase 2: each slice ends with something the owner can do on a phone, each leaves `main` coherent (nothing half-wired is reachable), each reuses the reference's screens and texts in Bull's look, and every deviation is listed where it lands.

### 2.0 Decisive facts for the order of the slices

1. **A published order can be taken at any moment, by anyone, and from the lock of the taker's bond the maker has `escrow_duration` to do its step, or loses its bond** (§ 1.10): 3 hours by default, 1 to 10 hours in the reference client, 30 minutes to 10 hours at the coordinator. So the first APK that publishes an order must already run the trade that follows, on that order's side. A maker buyer's side from 6 on is the phase 2 buyer's (payout, chat, « fiat sent », the end), with no escrow to pay; a maker seller's side adds the whole trade's escrow, possibly twice (fact 5).
2. **Cancelling is free at 0, 1 and 2** (§ 1.7). The cheapest trial with a paid bond is therefore: make a buy order at the coordinator's minimum, pay the maker bond, see the order public and in the book, cancel it. Nothing goes to the coordinator; paid from this wallet, what is lost is the swap's fees and network fee, and the refund comes back through the swap service after a delay (32 hours the one time a bond refund was observed, `AGENTS.md`, « The escrow paid from this wallet (#97) »).
3. **The take usually happens while the app is closed, and this app does not notify** (the owner's decision of 2026-11-16 puts the background service and the notifications at the end of the project). The reference rings and notifies (`Prompts/PublicWait.tsx:86-88`). Until the end, a maker's trial is safe only if the owner is the taker (the official app on another device) or keeps the app open, and every trial script says so (§ 7, proposal 1).
4. **The maker bond is held for up to about 51 hours with the defaults** (§ 1.4), by the swap service when it is paid from this wallet: longer than any hold tried so far; only one maker bond was paid through the service, cancelled before any take (the archive's `phase2/paiement-portefeuille/etude.md` § 2.2).
5. **A maker seller's order can come back to the book and ask a second escrow** (§ 1.10): the database of phase 2 pins the first escrow's payment hash for ever and records one payment per step, so it would take the second escrow for a breach (§ 3.5).
6. **The trade's sats are fixed by the coordinator at the take, at its price then** (§ 1.9): the 2 % check of the taker against the price seen just before the take has no equivalent anchor for a maker (§ 3.3).
7. **The range comes with the advanced options**: opening them switches the form to « Amount Range » by default (§ 1.11), so a form with advanced options and no range would not be the reference's.

### 2.1 Slice 1 — make a buy order at a fixed amount, publish it, cancel it, and run the trade if it is taken (APK 1)

What the owner can do: « Create » (enabled), the form without advanced options (« Buy », amount and currency, payment methods with « Add custom payment method », premium, the coordinator, the summary sentence, « Estimated Bond », « Order current rate », the reasons of a disabled button), « Create order », « Store your garage key », then on the trade screen « Lock N Sats to PUBLISH order » with « Pay with this wallet » or « Pay with another wallet », « Your order is public » with its lines, the order in the book, « Cancel » at 0 (with the reference's dialog) and at 1 (at once), « The order has expired » with its reason, and if the order is taken, the buyer's whole trade as phase 2 built it, with the maker's variants.

- **Form.** The reference's basic form, field by field and text by text (§ 1.11). « Sell » is shown disabled with « Selling as maker is coming in a later version. » (ours, as phase 2's « Selling is coming in a later version. »), so that no maker locks a bond for an escrow the app cannot pay yet. « Enable advanced options » is shown disabled with « Coming in a later version. » (ours), so that the defaults apply: 23:59 public, 3 hours of escrow step, a bond of 3 %, a fixed amount (fact 7). The amount's limits, the rate and the estimated bond as the reference computes them (§ 1.3), from the chosen coordinator's `/api/limits/` and `/api/info/`, the size limit already ported. The default coordinator from the reference's lottery (§ 3.9). « Existing orders match yours! » comes in slice 2: until then the button reads « Create order » at once (a deviation for one slice).
- **Before sending.** No robot to choose: a fresh robot is reserved for every order (§ 7, proposal 2), so « Robot has an active order » and « Change Robot & Create Order » never show; « Store your garage key » as the take shows it (`StoreTokenDialog`).
- **Trade screen, maker.** Panels 0, 1 and 5 of § 1.11 with their titles and prompts; the step « (x/5) »; the bond line « Your maker bond is locked / unlocked / settled »; « Copy URL » and « Cancel » at 0, 1, 2, 6 and 7; at 1 the pause button is not drawn yet (slice 2) and « Public orders for … » and « Premium rank » are computed from the book this app reads, as the reference computes them from its own, its counting quirk included (§ 1.11). At 0 the maker bond's block is the bond block of phase 2 (#100), title aside: « Pay with this wallet » first, « Pay with another wallet » with the QR code and the copy, the reference's hold-invoice sentence word for word (#135). The « Order » tab gives the maker's « You send / You receive » with the maker's fee, as the reference does (`OrderDetails/index.tsx:193-196`).
- **After the take**, the phase 2 panels of a buyer from 6 to 18, unchanged but for: 17 « You have lost the dispute » and 18 « You have won the dispute » for a maker; « Cancel » at 6 and 7 with the reference's « bond at risk » dialog, as for a taker; « back in the book » (the taker seller did not lock the escrow, or cancelled) shown as the order public again, with the maker bond still locked, and the half of the taker's bond credited to the maker robot said in the rewards line the Garage already has.
- **Book.** An order this device made opens its trade screen, never « Take Order » (§ 3.7).
- **The money in this slice**: the maker bond (from this wallet or another), the risk of losing it once taken (fact 1, fact 3), the trade as buyer.
- **Not in this slice**: « Sell », advanced options (range, durations, bond size, description, password, location, « Swap? »), pause, renewal, « Existing orders match yours! ».

### 2.2 Slice 2 — advanced options, range, pause, renewal, similar orders (APK 2)

What the owner can do: « Enable advanced options » with « Amount Range » / « Exact Amount », « From » … « to », « Description », « Public Duration (HH:mm) », « Escrow/Invoice Timer (HH:mm) » (the owner can then choose 10 hours for his trials, fact 3), « Fidelity Bond Size »; pause and « Unpause Order » (« Your order is paused »); « Renew Order » at 5 and « Renew » at 13 to 15, each a new order with a new robot and a new bond; « Submit » then « Existing orders match yours! » before « Create order ». « Password » and « Face to Face Location » stay disabled in the advanced options until slice 4, « Swap? » is not drawn (§ 7, proposal 4).

- **Range**: the maker bond on the maximum (`api/logics.py:1266-1268`: a taker who takes less leaves the maker with more locked than its trade needs, said in the trial script), the amount taken checked within the bounds from 6 on, the reference's client ratios 1.6 and 14.8 in the form, the coordinator's 1.5 and 15 in the package.
- **Pause**: one tap, no dialog, as the reference; the request never sent twice (a toggle sent twice undoes itself, § 1.6): the use case reads the order first and sends only when the status it reads is the one the user saw (§ 3.1).
- **Renewal**: a new make from the stored terms of the expired order (§ 3.5), never from the coordinator's answer, with a fresh robot (§ 7, proposal 2), the summary and the bond estimate shown again before the bond is asked; « Renew » after a trade works, where the reference in Garage Key mode refuses (§ 1.8), since the robot changes anyway.
- **The money**: a renewal asks a new bond, so a new payment.

### 2.3 Slice 3 — make a sell order (APK 3)

What the owner can do: « Sell » in the form; once taken, « Lock N Sats as collateral » with « Pay with this wallet » (#97's block) or another wallet, the wait for the buyer's payout, the chat, « Confirm … received »; and when the buyer gives no payout in time, the escrow returned, the order back in the book, and **a new escrow** for the next taker, with its own payment record.

- The state machine's maker seller (edges of § 3.2), the second escrow (the trail's take count and the payment's take, § 3.5), the escrow's ceiling for a maker (§ 3.3), the texts of the escrow confirmation with the rate the sats imply (§ 3.3).
- **The money**: the whole trade, custodially with the swap service while the escrow is held (`AGENTS.md`, « The escrow paid from this wallet (#97) »), possibly twice at once when a first escrow's refund has not come back yet when a second taker asks one. The most sensitive slice of the phase.

### 2.4 Slice 4 — password and meeting point (APK 4)

What the owner can do: « Password » (its SHA-256 sent, never stored; asked again at a renewal and sent hashed, which corrects the reference's renewal, § 1.8), the order then absent from the book and shared by « Copy URL »; « Face to Face Location » with the map the book already draws and the reference's randomization of the point, opened by itself when a F2F method is chosen.

- **Privacy**: the location is sent to the coordinator and published by it with the order (`api/nostr.py:109-156`, a geohash tag): the reference's randomization is kept, and nothing of the device's own position is read (the map has no location permission, `AGENTS.md`, « The Map view of the book (#30) »).
- **Taking a protected order** is not part of phase 3: the two-step take refuses an order with a password (`take_order_usecase.dart:88`, `:317`), so a protected order made here can be taken with the official app only, said in the trial script (§ 7, proposal 3).

### 2.5 End of the phase

Before phase 3 is called done, the question of the background service and the notifications is put to the owner again, with what the trials of slices 1 to 3 showed (§ 7, proposal 1). No code is planned for it in phase 3.
