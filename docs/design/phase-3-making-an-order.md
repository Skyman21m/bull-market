Loops: as in phase 2, the cleaner runs every 5 s (`api/management/commands/clean_orders.py:114-120`) and the invoice follower too; the publication after the bond's lock is seen within seconds.

### 1.2 Creating: `POST /api/make/`

**The request.** The reference client builds the order from the form (`components/MakerForm/MakerForm.tsx:257-275`, `buildOrderAttributes`) and sends it with `Order.make` (`models/Order.model.ts:196-231`) to the coordinator chosen in the form, with the robot's full header, token hash, PGP keys and Nostr key (`models/Robot.model.ts:72-86`). The body: `type` (« Buy » sends 0, « Sell » 1: the form's `fav.type` is the book's filter, inverted by `fav.type === 0 ? 1 : 0`, `MakerForm.tsx:258`), `currency` (an id), `amount` (`null` for a range), `has_range`, `min_amount`, `max_amount`, `payment_method` (the methods joined by spaces, « not specified » when none), `is_explicit` (false: the field defaults so, `Order.model.ts:73`, and the form never sets it), `premium` (0 when the field is empty), `satoshis` (`null`: the current client offers no order priced in sats, although the coordinator still accepts one), `public_duration` and `escrow_duration` (seconds), `bond_size` (percent), `latitude`, `longitude`, `password` (the SHA-256 hex of the password, `MakerForm.tsx:273`, or `null`) and `description` (or `null`). The coordinator's alias picks the coordinator and is not sent.

**What the coordinator checks**, in this order (`api/views.py:90-216`): an authenticated robot (1036, `:99-100`, in practice unreachable: the view's `IsAuthenticated` permission, `:93`, answers 401 or 403 before the view runs); the body against `MakeOrderSerializer` (`api/serializers.py:587-627`), which applies the model's validators, or an **empty 400 with no error code** (`api/views.py:102-103`): premium −100 to 999 (`api/models/order.py:91-99`), public duration 597.6 s to 86,400 s (`:109-122`), escrow duration 1,800 s to 36,000 s (`:124-133`), bond size 2 to 15 % (`:135-146`), coordinates within their bounds (`:148-168`), password at most 2,000 characters (`:170-176`), description at most 240 (`:178-184`), payment method at most 70 (`api/serializers.py:592-597`); a book under `MAX_PUBLIC_ORDERS` (1037, 100 in `.env-sample:151`, `api/views.py:105-120`); an amount, or both bounds of a range (1039, 1038, `:149-160`); under a row lock on the robot, **one active order per robot** (409 with 1000 to 1003, `:170-176`, `api/logics.py:75-135`); the size in sats at the coordinator's price at that moment (`t0_satoshis`, `api/views.py:201`): 1004 too big, 1005 too small, and for a range 1006 (minimum above the maximum ÷ 1.5), 1007, 1008, 1009 (maximum above 15 times the minimum) (`api/logics.py:138-177`, sizes 20,000 to 500,000 sats by default, `:31-32`, `.env-sample:155-157`); and the country of a meeting point (1010, `api/logics.py:180-188`). Defaults fill what the body leaves out (`api/views.py:140-147`).

**The answer** is a 201 with `ListOrderSerializer` (`api/views.py:216`, `api/serializers.py:83-107`): `id`, `status` 0, `created_at`, `expires_at` (creation + 300 s), `type`, `currency`, `amount`, `has_range`, `min_amount`, `max_amount`, `payment_method`, `is_explicit`, `premium`, `satoshis`, `maker` and `taker` (the coordinator's **internal user ids**, not hash ids), `escrow_duration`, `bond_size`, `latitude`, `longitude`. It has no `is_maker`, no `public_duration`, no description and **no bond invoice**: the bond is made at the maker's first `GET /api/order/` (`api/views.py:373-379`). `api/AGENTS.md` (« Endpoints ») says the make « returns maker bond invoice »: the code disagrees, and the code wins.

After a 201 the reference client opens the order's page and, in Garage Key mode, publishes a Nostr event of account recovery (`models/Garage.model.ts:547-569`).

### 1.3 Limits, price and fees: `/api/limits/` and `/api/info/`

- `GET /api/limits/`, anonymous: per currency, `code`, `price` (the coordinator's price of a bitcoin), `min_amount` and `max_amount` (`MIN_ORDER_SIZE` and `MAX_ORDER_SIZE` at that price, `api/views.py:1177-1197`).
- `GET /api/info/`: `maker_fee` (`FEE` × `MAKER_FEE_SPLIT`, 0.025 % with the sample) and `taker_fee` (`api/views.py:1042-1045`, `.env-sample:107-110`), the default `bond_size`, `min_order_size` and `max_order_size` (whose code default, 250,000, differs from the limits' 500,000, `api/views.py:1052-1053`), and `devfund` (`:1039-1041`).
- The form's limits: the coordinator's `min_amount` and `max_amount` times (1 + premium), the maximum capped by the coordinator's size limit (`calculateSizeLimit`, `models/Coordinator.model.ts:110-127`, already ported as `CoordinatorSizeLimit`, `features/p2p/lib/src/domain/coordinator_size_limit.dart`), then a safety margin, minimum × 1.03 and maximum × 0.98 (`MakerForm.tsx:96`, `:140-158`). A range must have its maximum at least 1.6 and at most 14.8 times its minimum in the client (`components/MakerForm/AmountRange.tsx:54-55`), 1.5 and 15 at the coordinator.
- « Order current rate »: the limits' `price` × (1 + premium), to 5 significant digits (`MakerForm.tsx:160-169`). « Estimated Bond »: the amount (the maximum of a range) over that rate, in sats, times the bond size, rounded down (`utils/bondCalculator.ts:12-41`, `hooks/useBondEstimate.ts:26-39`).

### 1.4 The maker bond

- **Amount**: `int(last_satoshis × bond_size / 100)`, where `last_satoshis` is the order's sats at the coordinator's price when the maker's first read makes the invoice, the largest amount for a range (`api/logics.py:1266-1268`, `:262-275`). The next reads return the same invoice (`:1259-1263`).
- **Deadline**: the order's own, 300 s after the creation; a read after it expires the order and answers 1022 « Invoice expired. You did not confirm publishing the order in time. Make a new order. » once (`api/logics.py:1254-1256`, `api/errors.py:25`), then the order is at 5. The invoice itself is made for `t_to_expire(0)`, padded by 50 % by LND (`api/lightning/lnd.py:287-289`).
- **CLTV**: (24 h of chat + `escrow_duration` + `public_duration` + `EXP_TAKER_BOND_INVOICE`) × 1.7 ÷ 8 min a block (`api/logics.py:1229-1249`, `.env-sample:161-167`). With the reference client's defaults (86,340 s public, 10,800 s escrow, 200 s): 183,740 s × 1.7 ÷ 480 s ≈ **650 blocks**, about 4.5 days at the real 10 minutes a block; at the maximums (24 h, 10 h): 209,000 s × 1.7 ÷ 480 s ≈ **740 blocks**, about 5.1 days. That is the longest a stuck maker bond can stay locked, almost twice a taker bond's (about 344 blocks, the phase 2 note § 1.2).
- **How long it is held** in a normal life: from the lock to the end of the trade, that is the rest of the public duration, then the escrow step and the chat: about 51 hours with the client's defaults, 58 at the maximums; it is returned at « Confirm received » (`api/logics.py:1721-1726`), at a cancel at 1 or 2, at an expiry at 1 or 2, and settled at once when a dispute opens (`api/logics.py:486-510`).
- **Description**, shown by the paying wallet: « {node alias} - Publishing 'Order {id}: BUY|SELL BTC for {amount} {currency}' - Maker bond - This payment WILL FREEZE IN YOUR WALLET, check on the trading platform if the lock was successful. It will be unlocked (fail) unless you cheat or cancel unilaterally. », or « Payment reference: {uuid} … » for a stealth robot (`api/logics.py:1270-1273`, the order's text `api/models/order.py:303-308`).

### 1.5 Publication

When the maker bond locks, the invoice follower publishes the order (`follow_invoices.py:233-240`, `Logics.publish_order`, `api/logics.py:1205-1227`): status 1, `expires_at` = creation + `public_duration`, and for a range the amount cleared and `last_satoshis` computed again at the current price. The REST book lists the orders at 1 without a password (`api/views.py:776-790`), cached 10 s (`BOOK_CACHE_TTL`, `:85`); the coordinator also publishes a Nostr event of kind 38383 for the order, except for an order with a password or when it has no Nostr key configured (`api/nostr.py:25-33`), and the official client's live book comes from those events (`api/AGENTS.md`, « Redis response caching »). This app reads `/api/book/`.

### 1.6 Pause

`POST /api/order/` with `{"action": "pause"}` (`api/views.py:686-689`, `Logics.pause_unpause_public_order`, `api/logics.py:1758-1783`): 1 → 2 or 2 → 1, refused with 1047 for a robot that is not a participant (`api/views.py:617-619`, checked before the action), 1032 for the taker and 1033 at any other status; the answer is the read (`api/views.py:695`). **The body has no direction: the same request pauses and resumes**, so a request sent twice undoes itself. A pause moves no deadline, expires no pre-take, and a pre-taker's bond that locks while the order is paused is returned to it (`follow_invoices.py:243-261`, which finalizes only a public order). The reference client sends it from the pause button of status 1 and « Unpause Order » of status 2, with no confirmation (`TradeBox/index.tsx:310-313`).

### 1.8 Expiry and renewal

- Untaken, the order expires at creation + `public_duration`, public or paused, with its bond returned and no action needed (`api/logics.py:340-358`; « If the order expires untaken, your bond will return to you (no action needed). », `Prompts/PublicWait.tsx:95`).
- **Renewal is not an action of the backend**: « Renew Order » makes a new order with a new `POST /api/make/` from the expired order's own fields (`TradeBox/index.tsx:146-200`), which the coordinator gives a participant at 5, 13, 14 and 15 for that purpose (`public_duration`, `bond_size`, `api/views.py:490-498`; not at 15 to the robot being paid, § 1.1). It is a new order, with a new number and a new bond to pay. The maker sees « Renew Order » at 5 (`Prompts/Expired.tsx:40-97`, with « Password » and « Enter a password again » when the order had one) and « Renew » on the screens of 13, 14 and 15 (`Prompts/Successful.tsx:276-285`, `TradeBox/index.tsx:602-690`).
- In Garage Key mode the renewal uses the current robot only if it is « reusable », that is without an active order and with its last order cancelled or expired (`models/Slot.model.ts:293-309`); otherwise it shows « This robot already has an active order. Navigate to a new account to create another order. » or « This robot was involved in a trade. Please navigate to a new account to create orders. » (`TradeBox/index.tsx:150-157`). So « Renew » after a trade always shows the second text in that mode.
- **Two defects of the reference, read and not tried**: the password typed again at a renewal is sent as typed (`TradeBox/index.tsx:180`), where the form sends its SHA-256 (`MakerForm.tsx:273`) and a taker sends the SHA-256 of what it types (`OrderDetails/TakeButton.tsx:333`), so a renewed protected order would refuse its own password (1045); and « Renew » at 13 to 15 passes no password at all (`TradeBox/index.tsx:620-623`, `:643-646`, `:681-684`), so a protected order renewed after a trade would be public.

### 1.9 The take, as the maker lives it

1. A robot takes: a `TakeOrder` is made, the order stays at 1 (`api/logics.py:205-234`); several robots may pre-take at once, and the first whose bond locks wins, the others' bonds being returned (`follow_invoices.py:243-261`). The maker sees none of it (§ 1.1).
2. A pre-taker that does not lock in time, or cancels, changes nothing for the maker.
3. A taker's bond locks: `finalize_contract` (`api/logics.py:1321-1380`): the taker set, a range's amount set to the amount taken, **`last_satoshis` fixed at the coordinator's price at that moment** (`:1332-1335`), the deadline set to now + `escrow_duration` (`:1338-1340`), status 6. This is where the maker joins the trade that phase 2 built, as the buyer of a buy order or the seller of a sell order (`Logics.is_buyer`, `api/logics.py:236-254`). The trade's sats are fixed by the coordinator alone, at a moment the maker does not choose, possibly 24 hours after the creation.

### 1.10 After the take: maker buyer, maker seller

| | Maker **buyer** (buy order) | Maker **seller** (sell order) |
|---|---|---|
| Locks the escrow | the taker | **the maker**: the trade's sats plus the maker's fee (`Logics.escrow_amount`, `api/logics.py:733-749`) |
| Gives the payout | **the maker**: the trade's sats less the maker's fee (`Logics.payout_amount`, `:650-669`, the maker's fee at `:659-660`) | the taker |
| The maker's step not done in time | at 8, 5 « Invoice not submitted »: **the maker bond settled**, half of it to the taker, the escrow returned (`:423-443`) | at 7, 5 « Escrow not locked »: **the maker bond settled**, half of it to the taker (`:381-402`) |
| The taker's step not done in time | at 7: the taker's bond settled, half of it to the maker, **the order public again** with the maker bond still locked (`:404-421`) | at 8: the taker's bond settled, half of it to the maker, **the escrow returned**, the order public again (`:445-458`) |
| Nobody acted at 6 | both bonds settled, 5 (`:360-379`) | the same |
| The taker cancels at 6 or 7 | its bond settled, half of it to the maker, the order public again (`:1110-1130`) | the same; an escrow invoice made before is only detached (`publish_order`, `:1215-1220`), and a payment that locks it later is returned at once by the invoice follower's « Weird! » branch (`follow_invoices.py:270-280`) |
| The chat runs out | a dispute opens: the escrow and both bonds to the coordinator (`api/logics.py:460-468`, `:486-510`) | the same |
| The maker's fee | 0.025 % with the sample, taken from its payout | 0.025 %, added to its escrow |

**Back in the book** (the taker's default or cancel): the order is public again with **the same deadline as at the start**, creation + public duration (`api/logics.py:1207-1209`); when that deadline has passed, the cleaner expires the order within seconds and returns the maker bond (`clean_orders.py:48-70`, `api/logics.py:340-358`). The order can be taken again; for a maker seller the next take asks **a new escrow invoice**, since `publish_order` cleared the old one (`api/logics.py:1218`) and the invoice is made only when none is attached (`:1491-1495`).

**Rewards**: half of a bond the other side loses is credited to the robot's `earned_rewards` (`Logics.add_slashed_rewards`, `api/logics.py:1822-1880`, `SLASHED_BOND_REWARD_SPLIT`, `.env-sample:222`). When a range order is taken for less than its maximum, a maker that loses its bond loses only as much as the taker's bond, and the rest of its bond is credited back to it as rewards when above 100 sats (`api/logics.py:1836-1841`, `:1869`). Rewards are claimed with an invoice (`POST /api/reward/`, `api/views.py:1077-1108`), which this app does not do yet (phase 4).

## 2. What phase 3 delivers, slice by slice

Principle, as in phase 2: each slice ends with something the owner can do on a phone, each leaves `main` coherent (nothing half-wired is reachable), each reuses the reference's screens and texts in Bull's look, and every deviation is listed where it lands.

### 2.0 Decisive facts for the order of the slices

1. **A published order can be taken at any moment, by anyone, and from the lock of the taker's bond the maker has `escrow_duration` to do its step, or loses its bond** (§ 1.10): 3 hours by default, 1 to 10 hours in the reference client, 30 minutes to 10 hours at the coordinator. So the first APK that publishes an order must already run the trade that follows, on that order's side. A maker buyer's side from 6 on is the phase 2 buyer's (payout, chat, « fiat sent », the end), with no escrow to pay; a maker seller's side adds the whole trade's escrow, possibly twice (fact 5).
2. **Cancelling is free at 0, 1 and 2** (§ 1.7). The cheapest trial with a paid bond is therefore: make a buy order at the coordinator's minimum, pay the maker bond, see the order public and in the book, cancel it. Nothing goes to the coordinator; paid from this wallet, what is lost is the swap's fees and network fee, and the refund comes back through the swap service after a delay (32 hours the one time a bond refund was observed, `AGENTS.md`, « The escrow paid from this wallet (#97) »).
3. **The take usually happens while the app is closed, and this app does not notify** (the owner's decision of 2026-11-16 puts the background service and the notifications at the end of the project). The reference rings and notifies (`Prompts/PublicWait.tsx:86-88`). Until the end, a maker's trial is safe only if the owner is the taker (the official app on another device) or keeps the app open, and every trial script says so (§ 7, proposal 1).
4. **The maker bond is held for up to about 51 hours with the defaults** (§ 1.4), by the swap service when it is paid from this wallet: longer than any hold tried so far; only one maker bond was paid through the service, cancelled before any take (the archive's `phase2/paiement-portefeuille/etude.md` § 2.2).
5. **A maker's order can come back to the book and be taken again, usually while the app is closed** (§ 1.10): the app may read 6, 7 or 8, then 6, 7 or 8 again, of another contract, without ever seeing the order public in between. A maker seller is then asked a second escrow, a maker buyer a second payout invoice. The phase 2 records pin the first escrow's payment hash for ever and keep one payment per step, and the wallet's payout invoice is one per trade: as they stand, a second contract would be taken for a breach (§ 3.2, § 3.5).
6. **The trade's sats are fixed by the coordinator at the take, at its price then** (§ 1.9): the 2 % check of the taker against the price seen just before the take has no equivalent anchor for a maker, and a maker buyer is exposed from the first APK to receiving fewer sats than the market gives (§ 3.3).
7. **The range comes with the advanced options**: opening them switches the form to « Amount Range » by default (§ 1.11), so a form with advanced options and no range would not be the reference's.

### 3.2 Domain: the maker's trade and its state machine

- **Origin.** `TradeOrigin.madeHere` beside `takenHere` and `recovered` (`features/p2p/lib/src/domain/trade_record.dart:10-21`), written only by the make use case. A record of origin `madeHere` without an order id is a **pending make** (§ 3.4); `TradeRecord.orderId` stays an `int`: the repository gives a pending make as its own type (`PendingMake`), so that the reviewed users of `TradeRecord` never see a record without an order.
- **What the maker asked: `MakerTerms`.** Set once, before the request leaves, never read again from the coordinator: the network, the coordinator, the order type and the role it gives (**the maker of a buy order buys**, the opposite of `tradeRoleTaking`, `trade_terms.dart:14-17`), the currency, the fixed amount or the range, the premium, the payment method text, the bond size, the public and escrow durations, whether there is a description (and its text, § 3.5), whether there is a password (never the password nor its hash), the coordinates sent, the coordinator's price for the currency read from `/api/limits/` just before the request (`createdPrice`), and the maker fee of `/api/info/` (refused above 1 %, as the taker fee is, `TradeTolerances.takerFeeCeiling`, `trade_tolerances.dart:31`). As #177 wrote it: `MakerTerms` holds the `MakeOrderRequest` itself, so that the terms and what is sent have the very bounds of `tryCreate` and the conversion is the identity; the password and the coordinates come with #189, which adds them to the request, and until then the record writes `has_password` false and no coordinates, and a record with either is refused. As #189 wrote it: the terms keep the request without the password's hash (`MakeOrderRequest.kept`, which says there is a password and is not sendable), the record writes `has_password` and the coordinates, and the make sends the request it was given, equal to the terms'.
- **`TradeTerms` for both sides.** The terms the state machine checks keep one type, so that every use case that compares an answer with `terms.orderId`, `terms.coordinator`, `terms.network` or `terms.role` (the cancel, the chat step, the rating, the reader) is unchanged; what depends on the side moves into a sealed member: `TakenSide` (the amount taken, whether the order has a range, the sats of an order priced in sats, the book's price, the taker fee, and the figures computed from them; the order's premium, bond size and escrow duration, which both sides have, stay on `TradeTerms`, with the bond this robot locks and its ceiling, by side) or `MadeSide` (the `MakerTerms` only: what a take of a range chooses is the contract's, kept in the trail, since it changes at each contract, `api/logics.py:1210-1213`, `:1329-1330`). The two alternatives are worse: a separate `MakerTerms`-only state machine would duplicate every guard and the reader; widening today's fields with nullable maker fields would touch the taker's checks, reviewed in phase 2, at every line. Storage follows (§ 3.5): a taker's terms stay in `trade_terms`, a maker's go to a new `maker_terms`. ### 3.3 The trade's sats, fixed at the take: what the app checks (the study's « E4 »)

**The question.** For a taker, the sats of the trade are checked within 2 % of the sats of the amount at the book's price read just before the take (`trade_status.dart:577-584`, `TradeTolerances.price`), because the coordinator fixes them seconds or minutes later (`api/logics.py:1332-1335`). For a maker, they are fixed at the take, at the coordinator's price then, up to 24 hours after the creation, often while the app is closed (§ 1.9), and the coordinator checks no size at that moment (sizes are checked at the creation only, `api/views.py:201-205`, `api/logics.py:138-177`). Two different tools are at stake, which phase 2 has both of and which must not be confused: a **warning** (`TradeWarning`), which says the coordinator contradicted the terms, hides the QR code behind « Show anyway » and stops « Pay with this wallet » for the trade; and a **ceiling** of the wallet (`TradeTerms.bondCeiling` and `TakenSide.escrowCeiling`, `trade_terms.dart:409-416` and `:75-80`, given to `walletPaymentFor` as what the user accepted, `pay_bond_from_wallet_usecase.dart:284-304`, `wallet_payment.dart:75-77`), which only bounds what this wallet pays and says nothing about the coordinator.

**No warning on the price.** Reusing the taker's warning against the price at the creation would fire after any normal move of the market over a day, and would push a maker seller to pay its escrow blind from another wallet. The alternatives as warnings are no better: a wider tolerance is arbitrary and still fires; a fresh `/api/limits/` read at the first read of 6, 7 or 8 is the same coordinator's price at the moment the app looks, not at the take, and misfires when the app opens hours later; the coordinator's `price_now` last read at 1 or 2 runs only when the app was open around the take, needs a price stored in the trail, and like the previous one protects only against a coordinator that contradicts itself; a third party's price (the wallet's) is an invention, another index, and a new dependency on the shell. So, as the reference: no warning of the state machine compares `last_satoshis` with a price (the payment rule below, decided by the owner, compares the amount with the wallet's own rate at the confirmation of a payment, which is no `TradeWarning`). What stays checked, from 6 on, for a maker: the order's terms (`TradeTermsChanged`); the escrow invoice's amount equals `escrow_satoshis` equals `trade_satoshis` (`EscrowAmountMismatch`); the payout amount equals `trade_satoshis` (`PayoutAmountMismatch`); the implied fee, `escrow − last_satoshis` for a seller or `last_satoshis − invoice_amount` for a buyer, between 0 and `last_satoshis` × the maker fee of the terms + 1 sat (`TradeTolerances.feeSats`), the maker fee refused above 1 % at the creation.

### 3.3 bis The payment rule's three gaps (#237)

**1. When the rule cannot read the order's terms, and what the screen then says.**

- *Where.* The rule reads the terms once per trade screen, through `CheckPayoutRateUsecase.termsOf` (`domain/usecases/trade/check_payout_rate_usecase.dart:31-49`), which opens the trade from this device's records (`TradeReader.open`, `domain/usecases/trade/trade_reader.dart:202-271`, `_openMade` `:287-358`). For a buyer, `null` comes back when that opening is not `TradeOpened`; the cubit then emits « nothing to show » (`presentation/payout_rate_cubit.dart:234-238`), and since `_readTerms` keeps the answer for the cubit's life (`:311-317`), the silence lasts as long as the page stays open. The other `null`s (a seller, terms of the other role, an order priced in sats if `TakenRateRules.comparesOrdersPricedInSats` were ever `false`) are not this case: a seller's trade never reaches the rule (`_applies`, `:202`), terms of the other role are refused by `open` itself as another trade's (`trade_reader.dart:230-235`, `:317-321`), and the constant is `true`.
- *The real cases.* The trade screen shows a step only after its own watch opened the trade the same way (`domain/usecases/trade/trade_watch_session.dart:74-82`: `TradeWatchUnrestorable` or `TradeWatchUnavailable` otherwise, and no status). So the rule's opening fails, on a screen that shows 6, 8, 9 (or 15 below), only when this device's records give way between the two openings: the database refused the read (`_trails.historyOf`, `makerTermsOf`, a `P2pStorageFailure`), the vault refused to give the Garage Key (`InterimTradeRobots.robotOfSlot`, `domain/interim_trade_robots.dart:91-105`, read at each opening: a locked keystore), or the robot's binding could not be read (`trade_reader.dart:380-389`). A record that cannot be restored (`TradeNotRestorable`) or a coordinator gone from the federation list fail the watch's opening too, so the screen then shows no step. Every one is local: **no coordinator can cause it**, the opening sends nothing. Rare and passing, but silent today.
- *The words.* « Amount not checked » (« Montant non vérifié ») is true here: nothing was compared. Its validated second line, « Bull Wallet could not get its bitcoin price. », would be false (the price was never asked), and so would the validated explanation « Without this price, it cannot compare… ». So the bold line is kept and two sentences are proposed, to be validated by the owner: « Bull Wallet could not read this order's terms on this device. » (« Bull Wallet n'a pas pu relire les termes de l'ordre sur cet appareil. ») and, in the detail, « Without them, it cannot compare the coordinator's amount. » (« Sans eux, il ne peut pas comparer le montant du coordinateur. »).
- *« Retry ».* It can change something: it opens the trade's records again (the database, the vault, the binding: nothing sent, no network), and when the terms are read the step is checked as when it is first shown, which reads the Bull wallet's price as § 2.1 says (one request outside Tor, unless the last read is less than 3 minutes old). A failed reading is no longer kept for the page's life: it is tried again at « Retry » and when the step is shown anew (another step, the screen shown again, the app back), never at the watch's own cadence. « Submit » and « Confirm … sent » stay possible, the line shown.

**2. The rate of the creation and the rate of the take, for a currency the Bull wallet does not price.**

- *The rate of the creation* is `MakerTerms.createdRate` (`domain/trade/maker_terms.dart:299-305`): `createdPrice`, the price `/api/limits/` gave anonymously just before the make (`MakeOrderUsecase`, `domain/usecases/trade/make_order_usecase.dart:474`), times 1 plus the order's premium. It is written once with the pending make, before the make's request leaves, and the database refuses any update or delete of it (`maker_terms_set_once`, `maker_terms_never_deleted`, `data/maker_terms.drift`): **a coordinator cannot change it after the fact**. But it gave it: for a currency the Bull wallet does not price, nothing checked it at the creation (the form's line says only that the price is not checked, § 3.3, #181). A renewal is a new make, with new terms.
- *The rate of the take* is the contract's amount over its sats, `last_satoshis`, which a participant reads as `satoshis_now` from the take on (`api/views.py:324`; fixed at the taker bond's lock, `api/logics.py:1332-1335`), as `MakerTerms.rateGap` computes it (`maker_terms.dart:389-405`) and the state machine already does for 6 to 9 (`domain/trade/trade_status.dart:1644-1652`). It is the coordinator's word too, read at each read of the order; the state machine checks it against what is paid (the payout or the escrow less or plus the maker fee, `MakerFeeAboveAnnounced`, `trade_status.dart:1479-1486`).
- *What is measured.* The owner's threshold is on the sats that cost the user (§ 3.3: « a seller asked for more sats, a buyer promised fewer »), so the gap is measured the same way here: the contract's sats at the take against the contract's amount at the rate of the creation (`MakerTerms.createdSatoshis`, exact), in percent of the latter, the threshold `TradeTolerances.walletRateGap` reused, compared exactly. A buyer is warned when the sats fixed are more than 10 % fewer (the coordinator's rate more than 11.11 % higher); a seller when they are more than 10 % more (its rate more than 9.09 % lower). The N said is that sats gap, without its sign, in hundredths rounded away from zero, as every other gap of the rule (`WalletRateCompared.gapHundredths`), so that the alert never says 10.00 % or less. *Examples* (100 GBP, a currency the Bull wallet does not price, made at the coordinator's 50,000 GBP/BTC, premium 0: 200,000 sats at the creation's rate). Not the maker bond's confirmation at 0: there is no take yet, so no rate of the take, and the bond is already checked at 2 % against the coordinator's own estimate (`MakerTerms.bondAgrees`). Not a maker seller at 9: nothing of the rule is drawn there. **Never for an order taken here** (the owner's decision of 2026-11-24, `TakenRateRules.notPriced`): the grey line alone. Beside amounts that do not agree the rule draws nothing (`PayoutRateAmountsDisagree`, the state machine's alert alone), and this alert, computed from the same figures, follows that rule, with one exception: at 9, when this app kept the invoice asked at 6 or 8 and the state machine says the amounts do not agree (a `trade_satoshis` the reference never sends at 9, `api/views.py:345-356`), the kept invoice is compared alone (`PayoutRateAskedOnly`) and `againstOf` still computes the gap from `satoshis_now`, so the orange alert is drawn beside the state machine's alert: an alert beside an alert, never a line that reassures (functional review of #237, R5; not removed, an alert never silencing another); beside any other alert of the screen it is drawn (it is an alert), and it silences nothing (the grey line, the screen's alerts, a seller's own blocks stay).

### 3.5 Stored on the device: `p2p.sqlite` schema 8

Phase 2's tables are taker-shaped in their CHECKs, which SQLite cannot change in place: `trades` accepts the origins `takenHere` and `recovered` only and needs an order id (`features/p2p/lib/src/data/p2p_tables.dart:136-140`); `trade_terms` enforces the taker's role rule and holds a taker fee (`:203`, `:212`); `trade_trails` lists the taker's positions (`:274`), keeps the deadline of the last read only (`deadline`, `domain/trade/trade_storable.dart:167-168`) and its trigger pins the first escrow hash for ever (`trade_trails.drift:79`); `trade_payments` keeps one row per trade and step (`p2p_tables.dart:317`). Schema 8 is one step for all of it, in its own pull request before any maker code, so that the user's records are migrated once and reviewed once.

Every new CHECK and trigger is written so that a `NULL` never passes by accident: SQLite refuses a CHECK only when it is false, so a comparison with a nullable column is spelled out with `IS NULL` / `IS NOT NULL`, and triggers compare with `IS` / `IS NOT`, as today's do (`trades.drift:16-30`).

**The migration 7 → 8** follows the step 6 → 7 (`p2p_database.dart:153-173`): no transaction (the feature opens none), each statement done only when the file needs it, so that a step stopped after any of them runs again over the file it left; for each rebuilt table, its rows copied aside (`trades_of_schema7`, `trade_trails_of_schema7`, `trade_payments_of_schema7`), the table dropped (its triggers with it; a drop fires no trigger), made again, its rows copied back once each before its triggers exist (rows of closed trades included, which the insert triggers would refuse; the payments' `NOT EXISTS` comparing `take` too, 0 for every row copied), the triggers made again. **The three copies are kept until the very end** and dropped only after the foreign-key check below has passed: a step run again over a file it left finds each table already rebuilt (by its text, as 6 → 7 does), copies back from its copy only the rows still missing, and checks again, so no row is ever only in a table that a later statement drops. Then `maker_terms` and its triggers, then the replaced trigger of `trade_terms`: drift's `createTrigger` writes `CREATE TRIGGER IF NOT EXISTS` (`p2p_database.steps.dart:273`), which would leave the old one in place, so each replaced trigger is preceded by a constant `DROP TRIGGER IF EXISTS`, which makes the step safe to run again. The order: `trades` first, since the other tables' triggers and foreign keys name it. As #176 wrote it: the three tables are rebuilt and filled again first, then `maker_terms`, then every trigger of the step, so that no row is copied back while a trigger of the step exists; every trigger of each rebuilt table is dropped (`DROP TRIGGER IF EXISTS`) and all are made again together, in the order a new file has them, at every run of the step, since SQLite fires the triggers of one event newest first and the order decides which refusal a write meets (a first version made those of `trades` in another order, so that on a migrated file a pending take written again for a robot already recorded on an open order met `trades_one_open_per_order` before `trades_inserted_once`: functional review of #176); the foreign keys are read at the very start of the step, before any statement, and `PRAGMA foreign_key_check` runs on the whole file.

**The foreign keys must be off** while the step drops `trades`, the parent of five tables: with them on, `DROP TABLE` runs an implicit delete that the children's rows would make fail, at every opening. They are off by default and turned on only in `beforeOpen`, after any step (`p2p_database.dart:37-40`, `:176`), but no test checks it during a migration today (the migration tests check that they are on afterwards, `features/p2p/test/migrations_test/p2p_database/migration_test.dart:626`, `:969`). So the step reads `PRAGMA foreign_keys` and, when they are on, turns them off (`PRAGMA foreign_keys = OFF`, which SQLite's procedure for a rebuild uses, outside a transaction: drift opens none around a step; `beforeOpen` turns them on again after every step) and reads them again, refusing to drop `trades` only when they stayed on; it runs `PRAGMA foreign_key_check` on the whole file once every row is back, then drops the copies (as #176 wrote it, after its functional review; a first version refused at once when they were on). The SQLite the app ships on Android (`sqlite3_flutter_libs` 0.5.42, the prebuilt `sqlite3-native-library` 3.52.0) is built without `SQLITE_DEFAULT_FOREIGN_KEYS`, as its compile options read in an APK of this app show, so they are off when a step runs. What happens when either check fails, exactly: the step throws, drift does not write version 8, and the next opening runs the step again and fails the same way, at every opening. With the foreign keys stuck on, nothing was changed (the refusal comes before the first write); after a failed `foreign_key_check`, the tables are already rebuilt and the three copies are still there, holding every row as it was at schema 7. Either way the P2P tab cannot open its records and says so (`P2pStorageFailure`, the existing « records unreadable » message); the wallet, whose data is not in `p2p.sqlite`, is untouched. Neither case should happen: the app's own setup keeps the foreign keys off during a step, and the trades' foreign key to their bindings has been enforced since schema 3. If one does while a trade is running, the user finishes it with the official RoboSats app and the Garage Key, as for a phone lost (the phase 2 note § 4.5), and a corrected version of the step repairs the file from the copies; this is said in the release notes of the version that brings schema 8, and preferred to writing a schema 8 file with orphan rows.

The rows keep every value; the new columns take their defaults (`take` 0, `take_deadline`, `taker_hash_id` and `taken_amount` `NULL`): every trade before schema 8 is a taker's, for which they are never filled nor read (§ 3.2), so a taker's trade migrated in the middle of its chat goes on as before. Every statement is a constant pinned as written by `robot_bindings_architecture_test.dart`, as the schema 7 rebuild's are (`:1182-1220`), and none names a binding: the review of that test is the security review of the migration. drift's `TableMigration` is not used (experimental, needs an `ignore`), as in 6 → 7.

**A new contract, step by step**: at the first take the trail keeps contract 0 (its deadline D0, its taker T0) and pins escrow H1; the wallet pays it (payment row `escrow`, take 0). The buyer gives no payout, the order goes back to the book, H1 is returned at once by the coordinator, and someone takes again, all while the app is closed. The next read shows 6 with a deadline D1 later than D0: in one write, the trail moves to `take` 1, keeps D1 and the new taker, and pins the new escrow H2. « Pay with this wallet » is offered for H2 whatever H1 stands at, its confirmation saying, in red while H1 is not back, H1's sats and what will be out with H2 (the owner's decision of 2026-11-19), after a confirmation that says this is a new contract, under the payment row of take 1; H1's refund goes on under its own row (`BondPaymentLine` of #97). An escrow that changes within one contract, an escrow equal to one already paid, or a taker that changes under the same deadline is a breach, as in phase 2.

**Not stored**, as in phase 2: invoices, the coordinator's answers, the robot's pair, the chat; and, new: **the order's password**, neither in clear nor hashed (only whether there is one); the renewal asks it again, as the reference does.

### 3.5 bis Stored on the device: `p2p.sqlite` schema 9 (#204, #231, #233)

Three issues need the records to keep something more, and one schema step carries all of them, so that the user's records are migrated and reviewed once (`AGENTS.md`, « Database schema migration support »): what each escrow (and the bond) took out of the wallet, beyond a restart (#233); where the payout invoice of each contract came from (#204); and a trace of terms a coordinator showed otherwise and then put back (#231). None of the three exists in RoboSats: they are this app's own protections, kept sober.

**The migration 8 → 9**, idempotent as every step (a step stopped after any statement runs again over the file it left, `user_version` being written only once the step is done): each column added only while missing (`_hasColumn`, as schema 2); `trade_payouts` made only while missing (drift's `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`); every trigger of `trade_payouts` dropped (`DROP TRIGGER IF EXISTS`, constants), then the marks of the contracts already under way written, each only while missing (below), then the four triggers of `trade_payouts` made again in the order a new file has them, so that a step run again over a file it left meets no trigger of its own while it writes the marks; then the three new triggers of `trade_trails` and `trade_payments` (`CREATE TRIGGER IF NOT EXISTS`, each the newest of its table, as in a new file). No transaction (the feature opens none), no foreign key turned off (no table is dropped), no row of a schema 8 table copied, changed or deleted; drift's `TableMigration` is not used. Every statement is a constant of `P2pDatabase`, pinned by value, and the step's calls in their order, by `robot_bindings_architecture_test.dart`.

**The marks** (`unrecorded`): `INSERT INTO trade_payouts (…) SELECT key_fingerprint, robot_index, take, 'unrecorded', NULL, NULL, NULL FROM trade_trails` for every trail of a buyer at a position where the payout of its current contract may already have been given (any position but `start`, `makerBondStep`, `public`, `paused`, `bondStep`, `preTakeUnavailable`, `publicAgain`, `notAvailable`), closed trades included (where a mark changes nothing: a closed trade never shows « Confirm … sent », and the trigger keeps its mark as it is), `WHERE NOT EXISTS` such a row. Schema 8 did not record payouts, so such a contract's payout is unknown, and said so, never « given elsewhere »; a contract read after the step (a pending take at schema 8, an order still in the book, a next contract) has no mark, and its payout is recorded from its first submission. A submission after the step replaces the mark of its contract (`unrecorded` → `thisWallet` or `pasted`), never the reverse.

**#233, step by step.** `PayBondFromWalletUsecase.payBond` writes the payment's start with `TradePaymentRepository.recordStart(…, sats: accepted.total)`: one statement, the `INSERT` of a first attempt (`confirmed_sats` the confirmed total) or the `UPDATE` of a new attempt of the same invoice (`confirmed_sats = CASE WHEN confirmed_sats IS NULL THEN NULL ELSE max(confirmed_sats, ?) END`: never lowered, since an earlier attempt that may hold funds is never forgotten, and an amount never known stays unknown), committed and synced to disk (`synchronous = FULL`) **before** `P2pWalletPort.payInvoice` is called; the wallet is asked only once that statement returned its row. Killed between the two, the row holds the amount and the wallet was asked nothing (`findPayment` finds no order: `EarlierEscrowCase.back`, which counts nothing); killed after, the amount is on disk with the payment. A write refused (a database that cannot be written, a total out of the column's bounds) pays nothing (`BondPayNotRecorded`). `TradePaymentRecord.confirmedSats` carries it; `EarlierEscrow.of` takes an earlier escrow's sats from its record, so `EscrowAmounts` (in memory, #187) is removed. After a restart the alert gives the exact total; « at least … sats » stays only for a payment recorded before schema 9 (`EscrowContract.notBackUnknown`), the rule unchanged: never a total below what is known, an alert and never a refusal.

**#204, step by step.** A payout is « given from this wallet » when the text submitted is an invoice or an address this wallet gave during this run of the app (`PayoutFromWalletUsecase`, which keeps, in memory only, each one it put in the field; compared without case for an invoice, BOLT11 being case-insensitive); anything else submitted is « pasted », whatever wallet it came from. `SubmitPayoutUsecase` writes the trace of the contract the payout was signed for (the `take` of the fresh read it checked and signed from) right after the coordinator's answer, before its read after: for an answer that took it (`PayoutGiven`) and for an outcome unknown (`PayoutOutcomeUnknown`, which may have been taken), never for a refusal, whose invoice the coordinator does not hold (`update_invoice` attaches only a valid one, `api/logics.py:835-947`). A write that fails is reported as the other records' (`recordProblem`), and its absence later says « not given from Bull Market », the side that alerts. The coordinator never gives the buyer's invoice back in a read (it gives the amounts to ask, `api/views.py:345-371`, `:470-488`), so the trace is this device's own memory, the only way to know. `TradeReader` reads the trace of the trail's contract into `TradeSeen.payout` for a buyer, at the opening of a trade and after each answer kept. Before « Confirm {amount} sent » (the chat step and its dialog), for a buyer: from this wallet, nothing; pasted, one alert line (« pasted: the sats will go to the wallet that made it »); no trace, one alert line (« not given from Bull Market: check where the sats will arrive »); `unrecorded` or records that cannot be read, one alert line (« Bull Market cannot tell whether it was given from this wallet »). Nothing is blocked. Where the payment rule already says in red that this app never saw the contract's payout invoice (`p2pPayoutRateNotSeen`, a contract seen late), this line is not said beside it: an alert is said alone, and the red one says more (functional review of schema 9, C2). The payout invoice's sats are still not kept: after a restart the rule's comparison at 9 is the coordinator's own figure, as § 3.3 says (« What it does not protect »). **The taker buyer is concerned the same way**: its single contract's payout may have been given from another client with the same Garage Key, or before a trade was taken over after recovery, so the same rule applies to every buyer.

**#231: the two questions of the issue.** Where the trace is kept: in the trail, three columns written by the same statement as the trail of the read that showed other terms (atomic with it, as `seen_late` and `earlier_escrows`: the feature opens no transaction, and a trace in a table of its own could be lost, or written without its trail, by a kill between two writes); in memory it would be lost at a restart. Which other terms a coordinator can change and put back without a trace: every term `TradeTermsChanged` compares on a status that is kept (for a taker the currency, the type, the bond size, the escrow duration, the sats of an order priced in sats, the premium, the fixed amount, the range and the amount taken; for a maker also the payment method text, the password's presence, the meeting point, the description and the public duration); a maker's contract amount read otherwise at 6 or later is refused as a whole (a breach, kept for good already). One rule covers them all in the same columns: the first read kept with `TradeTermsChanged` sets `terms_changed_at` to its read time, kept to the end; and two columns keep **the lowest and the highest premium** that reads kept showed otherwise than the trade's own (its terms kept here, beyond the tolerance), each written as soon as a premium differs, even after another term changed first; the lowest only goes down, the highest only goes up, neither is cleared, the lowest is never above the highest, and both exist only with `terms_changed_at` (an order not priced in sats, a premium within −100 to 999, as the package bounds it). As the security review of schema 9 asked (C1): a first trace of 2.01 % for an order's 2 %, then 99 %, said « 2.01 % » after the fact, less than was seen. As its verification asked (R8): one premium « farthest from the trade's own » forgot the side that costs the user (for a buyer a premium above the order's costs more, for a seller one below), and of two premiums as far on both sides it kept the first; −10 % then +7 % for an order's +2 % said −10 % alone, hiding the higher price a buyer was shown. Two extremes say both sides, need no subquery of the terms in the trigger (each column is compared with its own old value only) and read as they are: « only goes down », « only goes up ». The state machine sets them (`TradeTrail.termsChangedAt`, `termsChangedPremiumLow`, `termsChangedPremiumHigh`, in the two places that build the trail of an order read), `TradeTrailRecord.whyNotReplace` says the trigger's rule (`TrailReplacementRefusal.termsTraceChanged`), `TradeTrail.restore` checks it. It is information, not a `TradeWarning`: it does not forbid « Pay with this wallet » once the terms are back, as before #231; the trade screen says it in one alert line, under the state machine's warnings, at every status until the trade ends, unless the status shown carries `TradeTermsChanged` itself, from the first change and with the trade's own premium from its terms kept here, never the coordinator's read (security review of schema 9, R1): above only, « {coordinator} showed this trade with other terms from {day} at {time}, up to a premium of {high} instead of {own}. »; below only, « …, with a premium down to {low} instead of {own}. »; on both sides, « …, with premiums from {low} to {high} instead of {own}. »; without the terms at hand, the premiums shown alone; or, when no read showed another premium, « {coordinator} showed this trade with other terms from {day} at {time}. » (the functional review of schema 9 asked a short line, R3); no longer once the trade ended; the dialog of « Confirm … sent » says it too. What the rule does not cover: the value of a term other than the premium (only its time), the time of a premium changed after another term was (the time said stays the first change's), checks that are no terms (an amount that does not add up for one read, `DeadlineMovedBack`), and a change put back before the step (a schema 8 trail has no trace).

**Still not stored**, as in schema 8: invoices (the payout invoice included, and its payment hash), addresses, preimages, the coordinator's answers, its texts, the robot's pair, the chat, the password.

### 4.2 Never logged or stored

- Never logged, printed or put in a fixture, as in phase 2 (the phase 2 note § 4.3): the token and its hash, the header, the invoices, the payment hashes, coordinator text; and, new, the make's body: the amount, the premium, the payment methods, the description, the coordinates, the password and its hash.
- Never stored: the order's password, in clear or hashed (only whether there is one).
- Sent outside Tor from the create form (#181, § 3.3, « The price checked before the make »): the Bull wallet's price of the order's currency, only while an amount is typed, at the moments listed there; since #183, for a range, while its maximum is above zero, which includes the moment « Enable advanced options » opens on a range taken from the coordinator's limits with no amount typed (the user is then preparing an order; kept, rather than waiting for a bound to be typed, so that the comparison is on screen with the range the user sees; security review of #183, R2). It tells the price service the IP address, the time and the currency, even when no order is made; orders being public (the book, Nostr) with their currency, amount and time, the price service alone could tie an IP address to an order by the time for a currency little traded. Over Tor, on the book's anonymous sessions, opening the form reads `/api/info/` of every coordinator, then `/api/limits/` of the host chosen: a sign of an intent to make, which every coordinator sees, before the host sees the same session read them again just before a fresh robot's first contact (the make's own reads, #179).
- Sent outside Tor, as #180 wrote it (the owner accepted it on 2026-11-17): the wallet's price of a bitcoin in the order's currency, read from `api.bullbitcoin.com` (`api05.bullbitcoin.dev` on testnet) unless the wallet's one-minute cache holds it; the request carries the currency's code and the user's IP address, nothing of the trade, and leaves just after the swap service's quote (which gives that service the bond's amount) and before the confirmation, once more at each « Retry ». Were one operator to see both services, it could tie the IP address, the bond's amount and the order's currency together, the currency being what this request adds (an assumption of the security review of #180, not established in the code); § 3.3.
- Sent outside Tor since #246, only once « Show tiles » is turned on and its dialog acknowledged on a map: the tiles in view, from the feature's own client to `tile.openstreetmap.org` (the owner's decision of 2026-11-23), with the phone's IP address and so the area looked at, the name asked of the phone's resolver in clear; aiming at the meeting point on the real map undoes much of the point's randomization for whoever holds the provider's logs (`docs/design/phase-3-map-tiles.md` § 5).
- Clipboard: « Copy URL » puts the coordinator's address and the order's number in the clipboard on the user's tap only (`CancelButton.tsx:38-43`), as the phase 2 note § 4.3 allows for invoices: public, not cleared.
- Stored, in `maker_terms` only, never logged: what the maker asked (§ 3.5), the description and the coordinates included, which the coordinator holds and shows anyway; they are the user's own order, kept for the renewal and the terms' checks.

### 4.3 The rule for showing or paying an invoice

The phase 2 rule (the phase 2 note § 4.4) applies unchanged, with two cases more:

- **The maker bond at 0**: shown and offered to this wallet only from a stored status of a confirmed make (its order id read and this robot shown as its maker); its amount equal to `bond_satoshis` and within 2 % of the bond at the creation's price (§ 3.2); its payment hash pinned for the whole life of the order, and refused when another row of `trade_payments` already holds it, before the wallet is asked (#180, `trade_payments_hash_once`); payable until the earlier of its expiry and the order's deadline (creation + 300 s), less `TradeTolerances.walletPaymentMargin`; paid within what the user confirmed, under the step `bond`, after a confirmation that compares it with the wallet's own price (§ 3.3, as #180 wrote it).
- **A maker seller's escrow**: pinned and recorded per contract (§ 3.2, § 3.5): a new escrow is shown and paid only in the contract the trail has moved to, whether or not the app saw the order back in the book. « Pay with this wallet » pays it only under the owner's payment rule of § 3.3, within what the user confirms (the sats, the amount, the rate they imply and its gap from the creation's); for a contract after the first, as #187 wrote it after its security review and the owner's decision of 2026-11-19, whatever the earlier escrows of the order paid from this wallet stand at: back (refunded, or nothing sent) they are not said; not back yet (a refund pending, being prepared or « creating », sent, pending at the service, under review, failed or expired with funds that may have left, a state that cannot be told) their sats and what will be out with this one are said in red; kept by the coordinator (paid), said apart; under the payment's lock they are read again with the quote about to be paid, and more out than confirmed (a dearer quote included) asks a new confirmation; the total said is the collaterals', bond not included. An escrow that changes within one contract, or whose payment hash was already paid, is a breach, as in phase 2.

## 7. Open decisions

Proposals 1 to 10, and 22 added after the security review of #177, are put to the owner in plain words, with the default this note proposes; 11 to 21 are technical, with the recommendation first. None of them is decided by this note; the owner decided 7 and 22 on 2026-11-17 (§ 3.3).

**For the owner:**

1. **Being taken while the app is closed.** Example: you publish a buy order in the evening; someone takes it at 2 a.m.; with the default settings you have until 5 a.m. to give your invoice, otherwise you lose your bond. The official app rings; this one does not, since the background service and the notifications are kept for the end (your decision of 2026-11-16). Default: keep that decision; say the risk in this note and in every trial script; for the trials, be the taker yourself with the official app, or keep the app open, or choose the 10-hour timer once slice 2 offers it; ask the question again before phase 3 is called done.
2. **One new robot for every order, the renewal included.** Example: your order expires untaken; « Renew Order » makes it again, under another robot name and picture, and asks the bond again. The official app keeps the same robot when it can. Default: a new robot each time, since the app already never uses a robot twice and RoboSats itself advises one robot per trade; « Renew » then also works after a trade, where the official app asks you to switch robot.
3. **The order password.** Example: you want to sell to a friend; you set a password, the order stays out of the book, and you send your friend the link and the password; your friend uses the official app or, since #245, this one (« Search order » with the link). Default: offer it as RoboSats does, in the last slice; taking a protected order with this app was left for later, and came with #245.
4. **The « Swap? » mode** (Lightning sats against on-chain bitcoin instead of a currency). Default: not in phase 3, said as a deviation; the wallet already swaps.
5. **« Pay with this wallet » for the maker bond from the first slice.** Example: for an order of 50,000 sats, a bond of 1,500 sats goes to the swap service and can stay there about two days if the order is taken, plus the refund's delay (32 hours once). Default: yes, as for the taker bond (your decision of 2026-11-13 for « Pay with this wallet »), with these figures in the trial script and small amounts.
6. **The sentence « This box will ring 🔊 once a robot takes your order »**, which this app cannot keep. Default: the reference's sentence word for word, as for the hold-invoice sentence (#135), the limit said in the trial scripts; the other way is a line of ours under it, « Bull Market does not ring yet: open it to see whether your order was taken. ».
7. **Closed, decided by the owner on 2026-11-17** (§ 3.3: the wallet's rate and one threshold of 10 %; no factor F). As first put: **The amount fixed at the take, and how much this wallet may pay for it.** The sats of your trade are fixed by the coordinator when someone takes your order, at its price at that moment, maybe a day after you made it. Default: as RoboSats, no alarm compares that price with anything (the market moves, and a false alarm would stop « Pay with this wallet »); but before you pay a sell order's collateral, give your invoice or confirm you sent the money, a line shows the rate the coordinator fixed and how far it is, in %, from the rate when you made the order; and « Pay with this wallet » pays a collateral only up to a factor of the order's value at the creation's rate (the reviewer proposes 2, that is twice the value; above it, only « Pay with another wallet »). Your choice: the factor (1.5, 2, or no ceiling at all, as the first version of this note proposed). Example: you sell 50,000 sats' worth; with a factor of 2 this wallet never locks more than about 100,000 sats for it; a lying coordinator could still ask more, from another wallet only (§ 3.3).
8. **Cancelling a public order paid from this wallet.** RoboSats cancels a public or paused order at once, with no question. Default: the same, no dialog; after the cancel, the line under the trade says that the refund is on its way through the swap service (it exists since #100). The other way, a dialog with one line of ours on the swap's fees and the refund's delay, changes nothing of what happens, since the fees are spent and the delay the same whether you cancel or the order expires.
9. **Making a second order while one is active.** Today the middle entry of the P2P tab is « Order » while a trade is active, as the official app's button shows the current robot's order. In the official app you switch robot in the Garage to make another; here every order has its own robot. Default: keep « Order », and add « Create order » at the top of the « Active trades » sheet it opens (a button of ours, in the pull request that enables « Create »).
10. **The order of the slices.** Default: buy orders first, with the basic form (APK 1); the advanced options with the range, pause, renewal and similar orders next (APK 2); sell orders (APK 3); password and meeting point (APK 4).

## 8. What could not be established from the sources on disk

- Each coordinator's real settings: `EXP_MAKER_BOND_INVOICE`, `MAX_PUBLIC_ORDERS`, `FEE` and `MAKER_FEE_SPLIT`, `MIN_ORDER_SIZE` and `MAX_ORDER_SIZE`, `GEOBLOCKED_COUNTRIES`, `SLASHED_BOND_REWARD_SPLIT`, and whether it has a Nostr key configured (without one it publishes no order event, `api/nostr.py:32-33`, and the official app's live book would not show the order): only the defaults are on disk; `/api/info/` and `/api/limits/` give some of them.
- The backend version each coordinator runs. The reference itself moved from commit ff1998c to babf62e with no protocol change (§ 6, issue 0, #174).
- That status 3 never reaches a maker, and the two defects of the reference's renewal (§ 1.8): read in the code, not observed.
- The swap service: how long it keeps a payment pending, the longest CLTV it accepts (a maker bond of about 650 blocks was accepted once), what it does with a payment held a day or more, its refund delays (one observation, 32 hours), and how it handles two payments of one user held at once.
- Whether the official Android app behaves as the web frontend for a maker: read from `frontend/src`, which the Android app wraps, not observed.
- The matching of « Existing orders match yours! » (`utils/filterOrders.ts`): read line by line by #186 (§ 1.11, § 2.2); its defect (a range's bounds never searched) and the number of rows its table shows are read in the code, not observed in the official app.
- How long a new order takes to appear in a coordinator's REST book (a 10-second cache, § 1.5) and in the official app's: not measured.
- The reward claim (`POST /api/reward/`, `api/logics.py:1886-2022`): not read line by line, as in phase 2; it is phase 4.
- A corner case read in the code, not dangerous for money: the invoice follower publishes the order as soon as the maker bond locks, without looking at the order's status (`follow_invoices.py:233-240`), so an order expired at 0 whose bond invoice failed to be cancelled could become public again while this app's trail holds it ended; the expiry cancels that invoice (`api/logics.py:329-338`), which makes it improbable.
- Whether a coordinator ever changes a contract's deadline at 6, 7 or 8 outside the code read here (its admin actions, `api/admin.py`, were not read for it): the contract rule of § 3.2 assumes it does not.
- What the wallet's records say of an escrow's swap while the coordinator holds it, and how soon they say it failed once the coordinator returns it: the rule of § 4.3 relies on the states #97 shows, not measured for an escrow yet.
