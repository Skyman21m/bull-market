**Status: design**, written on 2026-11-16, before the code of phase 3 ([#13](https://github.com/Skyman21m/bull-market/issues/13)), without any code, commit or request to a coordinator. Where the code later differs, the code and its pull requests are right, and this note is updated. It follows the design note of phase 2 (`docs/design/phase-2-taking-an-order.md`, « the phase 2 note » below), whose rules and vocabulary it keeps: everything a maker shares with a taker from status 6 on is the phase 2 code, and this note says only what changes.

Every proposal of this note is a proposal: the decisions taken by the owner that it relies on are named with their date, and everything else is listed in § 7 with its default answer.

## 1. The reference flow, as maker

Reference: RoboSats/robosats at commit ff1998c (`ff1998caed30888b2fb60e15022c29063a60f1ba`, the reference of `AGENTS.md`), as for phase 2. Backend paths are relative to its root (`api/…`), frontend paths to `frontend/src/`. Where the backend's own agent notes disagree with the code, the code wins and the disagreement is noted. The reference was not refreshed for this note (`AGENTS.md`, « Refreshing the RoboSats reference », asks it before each phase starts, which needs the network): that is the first ticket of § 6.

**Status 3 never reaches the maker** (the question of the study): the coordinator stores no status 3, and its read shows 3 only to a robot whose own `TakeOrder` is live (`take_order` is filtered on `taker=request.user`, `api/views.py:248-250`, and the injection asks for it, `:381-386`); the maker's `TakeOrder` cannot exist (`validate_already_maker_or_taker`, `api/logics.py:75-100`, refuses with 1000 any robot already maker of an order at 0 to 3, 6 to 11 or 16, and the take is sent only after that check, `api/views.py:572-584`). While a robot prepares its bond, the order stays at 1 in the database, the maker reads 1, and no field of its read says that a pre-take exists. The maker goes from 1 straight to 6 when a taker's bond locks (`follow_invoices.py:243-251`, `Logics.finalize_contract`, `api/logics.py:1321-1380`). The reference client's « A taker has been found! » (`TradeBox/index.tsx:422-438`, `Prompts/TakerFound.tsx:12-15`) is therefore unreachable against this backend; this reading of the code was not observed on a coordinator. The phase 2 parser already refuses status 3 for anyone but an unnamed pre-taker with its bond (`packages/robosats/lib/src/coordinator/coordinator_order.dart:857-860`).

Loops: as in phase 2, the cleaner runs every 5 s (`api/management/commands/clean_orders.py:114-120`) and the invoice follower too; the publication after the bond's lock is seen within seconds.

### 1.2 Creating: `POST /api/make/`

**The request.** The reference client builds the order from the form (`components/MakerForm/MakerForm.tsx:257-275`, `buildOrderAttributes`) and sends it with `Order.make` (`models/Order.model.ts:196-231`) to the coordinator chosen in the form, with the robot's full header, token hash, PGP keys and Nostr key (`models/Robot.model.ts:72-86`). The body: `type` (« Buy » sends 0, « Sell » 1: the form's `fav.type` is the book's filter, inverted by `fav.type === 0 ? 1 : 0`, `MakerForm.tsx:258`), `currency` (an id), `amount` (`null` for a range), `has_range`, `min_amount`, `max_amount`, `payment_method` (the methods joined by spaces, « not specified » when none), `is_explicit` (false: the field defaults so, `Order.model.ts:73`, and the form never sets it), `premium` (0 when the field is empty), `satoshis` (`null`: the current client offers no order priced in sats, although the coordinator still accepts one), `public_duration` and `escrow_duration` (seconds), `bond_size` (percent), `latitude`, `longitude`, `password` (the SHA-256 hex of the password, `MakerForm.tsx:273`, or `null`) and `description` (or `null`). The coordinator's alias picks the coordinator and is not sent.

**What the coordinator checks**, in this order (`api/views.py:90-216`): an authenticated robot (1036, `:99-100`, in practice unreachable: the view's `IsAuthenticated` permission, `:93`, answers 401 or 403 before the view runs); the body against `MakeOrderSerializer` (`api/serializers.py:587-627`), which applies the model's validators, or an **empty 400 with no error code** (`api/views.py:102-103`): premium −100 to 999 (`api/models/order.py:91-99`), public duration 597.6 s to 86,400 s (`:109-122`), escrow duration 1,800 s to 36,000 s (`:124-133`), bond size 2 to 15 % (`:135-146`), coordinates within their bounds (`:148-168`), password at most 2,000 characters (`:170-176`), description at most 240 (`:178-184`), payment method at most 70 (`api/serializers.py:592-597`); a book under `MAX_PUBLIC_ORDERS` (1037, 100 in `.env-sample:151`, `api/views.py:105-120`); an amount, or both bounds of a range (1039, 1038, `:149-160`); under a row lock on the robot, **one active order per robot** (409 with 1000 to 1003, `:170-176`, `api/logics.py:75-135`); the size in sats at the coordinator's price at that moment (`t0_satoshis`, `api/views.py:201`): 1004 too big, 1005 too small, and for a range 1006 (minimum above the maximum ÷ 1.5), 1007, 1008, 1009 (maximum above 15 times the minimum) (`api/logics.py:138-177`, sizes 20,000 to 500,000 sats by default, `:31-32`, `.env-sample:155-157`); and the country of a meeting point (1010, `api/logics.py:180-188`). Defaults fill what the body leaves out (`api/views.py:140-147`).

**The answer** is a 201 with `ListOrderSerializer` (`api/views.py:216`, `api/serializers.py:83-107`): `id`, `status` 0, `created_at`, `expires_at` (creation + 300 s), `type`, `currency`, `amount`, `has_range`, `min_amount`, `max_amount`, `payment_method`, `is_explicit`, `premium`, `satoshis`, `maker` and `taker` (the coordinator's **internal user ids**, not hash ids), `escrow_duration`, `bond_size`, `latitude`, `longitude`. It has no `is_maker`, no `public_duration`, no description and **no bond invoice**: the bond is made at the maker's first `GET /api/order/` (`api/views.py:373-379`). `api/AGENTS.md` (« Endpoints ») says the make « returns maker bond invoice »: the code disagrees, and the code wins.

After a 201 the reference client opens the order's page and, in Garage Key mode, publishes a Nostr event of account recovery (`models/Garage.model.ts:547-569`).

### 1.3 Limits, price and fees: `/api/limits/` and `/api/info/`

- `GET /api/limits/`, anonymous: per currency, `code`, `price` (the coordinator's price of a bitcoin), `min_amount` and `max_amount` (`MIN_ORDER_SIZE` and `MAX_ORDER_SIZE` at that price, `api/views.py:1177-1197`).
- `GET /api/info/`: `maker_fee` (`FEE` × `MAKER_FEE_SPLIT`, 0.025 % with the sample) and `taker_fee` (`api/views.py:1042-1045`, `.env-sample:107-110`), the default `bond_size`, `min_order_size` and `max_order_size` (whose code default, 250,000, differs from the limits' 500,000, `api/views.py:1052-1053`), and `devfund` (`:1039-1041`).
- The form's limits: the coordinator's `min_amount` and `max_amount` times (1 + premium), the maximum capped by the coordinator's size limit (`calculateSizeLimit`, `models/Coordinator.model.ts:110-127`, already ported as `CoordinatorSizeLimit`, `features/p2p/lib/src/domain/coordinator_size_limit.dart`), then a safety margin, minimum × 1.03 and maximum × 0.98 (`MakerForm.tsx:96`, `:140-158`). A range must have its maximum at least 1.6 and at most 14.8 times its minimum in the client (`components/MakerForm/AmountRange.tsx:54-55`), 1.5 and 15 at the coordinator.
- « Order current rate »: the limits' `price` × (1 + premium), to 5 significant digits (`MakerForm.tsx:160-169`). « Estimated Bond »: the amount (the maximum of a range) over that rate, in sats, times the bond size, rounded down (`utils/bondCalculator.ts:12-41`, `hooks/useBondEstimate.ts:26-39`).

### 1.4 The maker bond

- **Amount**: `int(last_satoshis × bond_size / 100)`, where `last_satoshis` is the order's sats at the coordinator's price when the maker's first read makes the invoice, the largest amount for a range (`api/logics.py:1266-1268`, `:262-275`). The next reads return the same invoice (`:1259-1263`).
- **Deadline**: the order's own, 300 s after the creation; a read after it expires the order and answers 1022 « Invoice expired. You did not confirm publishing the order in time. Make a new order. » once (`api/logics.py:1254-1256`, `api/errors.py:25`), then the order is at 5. The invoice itself is made for `t_to_expire(0)`, padded by 50 % by LND (`api/lightning/lnd.py:287-289`).
- **CLTV**: (24 h of chat + `escrow_duration` + `public_duration` + `EXP_TAKER_BOND_INVOICE`) × 1.7 ÷ 8 min a block (`api/logics.py:1229-1249`, `.env-sample:161-167`). With the reference client's defaults (86,340 s public, 10,800 s escrow, 200 s): 183,740 s × 1.7 ÷ 480 s ≈ **650 blocks**, about 4.5 days at the real 10 minutes a block; at the maximums (24 h, 10 h): 209,000 s × 1.7 ÷ 480 s ≈ **740 blocks**, about 5.1 days. That is the longest a stuck maker bond can stay locked, almost twice a taker bond's (about 344 blocks, the phase 2 note § 1.2).
- **How long it is held** in a normal life: from the lock to the end of the trade, that is the rest of the public duration, then the escrow step and the chat: about 51 hours with the client's defaults, 58 at the maximums; it is returned at « Confirm received » (`api/logics.py:1721-1726`), at a cancel at 1 or 2, at an expiry at 1 or 2, and settled at once when a dispute opens (`api/logics.py:486-510`).
- **Description**, shown by the paying wallet: « {node alias} - Publishing 'Order {id}: BUY|SELL BTC for {amount} {currency}' - Maker bond - This payment WILL FREEZE IN YOUR WALLET, check on the trading platform if the lock was successful. It will be unlocked (fail) unless you cheat or cancel unilaterally. », or « Payment reference: {uuid} … » for a stealth robot (`api/logics.py:1270-1273`, the order's text `api/models/order.py:303-308`).

### 1.5 Publication

When the maker bond locks, the invoice follower publishes the order (`follow_invoices.py:233-240`, `Logics.publish_order`, `api/logics.py:1205-1227`): status 1, `expires_at` = creation + `public_duration`, and for a range the amount cleared and `last_satoshis` computed again at the current price. The REST book lists the orders at 1 without a password (`api/views.py:776-790`), cached 10 s (`BOOK_CACHE_TTL`, `:85`); the coordinator also publishes a Nostr event of kind 38383 for the order, except for an order with a password or when it has no Nostr key configured (`api/nostr.py:25-33`), and the official client's live book comes from those events (`api/AGENTS.md`, « Redis response caching »). This app reads `/api/book/`.

### 1.6 Pause

`POST /api/order/` with `{"action": "pause"}` (`api/views.py:686-689`, `Logics.pause_unpause_public_order`, `api/logics.py:1758-1783`): 1 → 2 or 2 → 1, refused with 1047 for a robot that is not a participant (`api/views.py:617-619`, checked before the action), 1032 for the taker and 1033 at any other status; the answer is the read (`api/views.py:695`). **The body has no direction: the same request pauses and resumes**, so a request sent twice undoes itself. A pause moves no deadline, expires no pre-take, and a pre-taker's bond that locks while the order is paused is returned to it (`follow_invoices.py:243-261`, which finalizes only a public order). The reference client sends it from the pause button of status 1 and « Unpause Order » of status 2, with no confirmation (`TradeBox/index.tsx:310-313`).

### 1.8 Expiry and renewal

- Untaken, the order expires at creation + `public_duration`, public or paused, with its bond returned and no action needed (`api/logics.py:340-358`; « If the order expires untaken, your bond will return to you (no action needed). », `Prompts/PublicWait.tsx:95`).
- **Renewal is not an action of the backend**: « Renew Order » makes a new order with a new `POST /api/make/` from the expired order's own fields (`TradeBox/index.tsx:146-200`), which the coordinator gives a participant at 5, 13, 14 and 15 for that purpose (`public_duration`, `bond_size`, `api/views.py:490-498`; not at 15 to the robot being paid, § 1.1). It is a new order, with a new number and a new bond to pay. The maker sees « Renew Order » at 5 (`Prompts/Expired.tsx:40-97`, with « Password » and « Enter a password again » when the order had one) and « Renew » on the screens of 13, 14 and 15 (`Prompts/Successful.tsx:276-285`, `TradeBox/index.tsx:602-690`).
- In Garage Key mode the renewal uses the current robot only if it is « reusable », that is without an active order and with its last order cancelled or expired (`models/Slot.model.ts:293-309`); otherwise it shows « This robot already has an active order. Navigate to a new account to create another order. » or « This robot was involved in a trade. Please navigate to a new account to create orders. » (`TradeBox/index.tsx:150-157`). So « Renew » after a trade always shows the second text in that mode.
- **Two defects of the reference, read and not tried**: the password typed again at a renewal is sent as typed (`TradeBox/index.tsx:180`), where the form sends its SHA-256 (`MakerForm.tsx:273`) and a taker sends the SHA-256 of what it types (`OrderDetails/TakeButton.tsx:333`), so a renewed protected order would refuse its own password (1045); and « Renew » at 13 to 15 passes no password at all (`TradeBox/index.tsx:620-623`, `:643-646`, `:681-684`), so a protected order renewed after a trade would be public.

### 1.9 The take, as the maker lives it

1. A robot takes: a `TakeOrder` is made, the order stays at 1 (`api/logics.py:205-234`); several robots may pre-take at once, and the first whose bond locks wins, the others' bonds being returned (`follow_invoices.py:243-261`). The maker sees none of it (§ 1.1).
2. A pre-taker that does not lock in time, or cancels, changes nothing for the maker.
3. A taker's bond locks: `finalize_contract` (`api/logics.py:1321-1380`): the taker set, a range's amount set to the amount taken, **`last_satoshis` fixed at the coordinator's price at that moment** (`:1332-1335`), the deadline set to now + `escrow_duration` (`:1338-1340`), status 6. This is where the maker joins the trade that phase 2 built, as the buyer of a buy order or the seller of a sell order (`Logics.is_buyer`, `api/logics.py:236-254`). The trade's sats are fixed by the coordinator alone, at a moment the maker does not choose, possibly 24 hours after the creation.

### 1.10 After the take: maker buyer, maker seller

| | Maker **buyer** (buy order) | Maker **seller** (sell order) |
|---|---|---|
| Locks the escrow | the taker | **the maker**: the trade's sats plus the maker's fee (`Logics.escrow_amount`, `api/logics.py:733-749`) |
| Gives the payout | **the maker**: the trade's sats less the maker's fee (`Logics.payout_amount`, `:650-669`, the maker's fee at `:659-660`) | the taker |
| The maker's step not done in time | at 8, 5 « Invoice not submitted »: **the maker bond settled**, half of it to the taker, the escrow returned (`:423-443`) | at 7, 5 « Escrow not locked »: **the maker bond settled**, half of it to the taker (`:381-402`) |
| The taker's step not done in time | at 7: the taker's bond settled, half of it to the maker, **the order public again** with the maker bond still locked (`:404-421`) | at 8: the taker's bond settled, half of it to the maker, **the escrow returned**, the order public again (`:445-458`) |
| Nobody acted at 6 | both bonds settled, 5 (`:360-379`) | the same |
| The taker cancels at 6 or 7 | its bond settled, half of it to the maker, the order public again (`:1110-1130`) | the same; an escrow invoice made before is only detached (`publish_order`, `:1215-1220`), and a payment that locks it later is returned at once by the invoice follower's « Weird! » branch (`follow_invoices.py:270-280`) |
| The chat runs out | a dispute opens: the escrow and both bonds to the coordinator (`api/logics.py:460-468`, `:486-510`) | the same |
| The maker's fee | 0.025 % with the sample, taken from its payout | 0.025 %, added to its escrow |

**Back in the book** (the taker's default or cancel): the order is public again with **the same deadline as at the start**, creation + public duration (`api/logics.py:1207-1209`); when that deadline has passed, the cleaner expires the order within seconds and returns the maker bond (`clean_orders.py:48-70`, `api/logics.py:340-358`). The order can be taken again; for a maker seller the next take asks **a new escrow invoice**, since `publish_order` cleared the old one (`api/logics.py:1218`) and the invoice is made only when none is attached (`:1491-1495`).

**Rewards**: half of a bond the other side loses is credited to the robot's `earned_rewards` (`Logics.add_slashed_rewards`, `api/logics.py:1822-1880`, `SLASHED_BOND_REWARD_SPLIT`, `.env-sample:222`). When a range order is taken for less than its maximum, a maker that loses its bond loses only as much as the taker's bond, and the rest of its bond is credited back to it as rewards when above 100 sats (`api/logics.py:1836-1841`, `:1869`). Rewards are claimed with an invoice (`POST /api/reward/`, `api/views.py:1077-1108`), which this app does not do yet (phase 4).

## 2. What phase 3 delivers, slice by slice

Principle, as in phase 2: each slice ends with something the owner can do on a phone, each leaves `main` coherent (nothing half-wired is reachable), each reuses the reference's screens and texts in Bull's look, and every deviation is listed where it lands.

### 2.0 Decisive facts for the order of the slices

1. **A published order can be taken at any moment, by anyone, and from the lock of the taker's bond the maker has `escrow_duration` to do its step, or loses its bond** (§ 1.10): 3 hours by default, 1 to 10 hours in the reference client, 30 minutes to 10 hours at the coordinator. So the first APK that publishes an order must already run the trade that follows, on that order's side. A maker buyer's side from 6 on is the phase 2 buyer's (payout, chat, « fiat sent », the end), with no escrow to pay; a maker seller's side adds the whole trade's escrow, possibly twice (fact 5).
2. **Cancelling is free at 0, 1 and 2** (§ 1.7). The cheapest trial with a paid bond is therefore: make a buy order at the coordinator's minimum, pay the maker bond, see the order public and in the book, cancel it. Nothing goes to the coordinator; paid from this wallet, what is lost is the swap's fees and network fee, and the refund comes back through the swap service after a delay (32 hours the one time a bond refund was observed, `AGENTS.md`, « The escrow paid from this wallet (#97) »).
3. **The take usually happens while the app is closed, and this app does not notify** (the owner's decision of 2026-11-16 puts the background service and the notifications at the end of the project). The reference rings and notifies (`Prompts/PublicWait.tsx:86-88`). Until the end, a maker's trial is safe only if the owner is the taker (the official app on another device) or keeps the app open, and every trial script says so (§ 7, proposal 1).
4. **The maker bond is held for up to about 51 hours with the defaults** (§ 1.4), by the swap service when it is paid from this wallet: longer than any hold tried so far; only one maker bond was paid through the service, cancelled before any take (the archive's `phase2/paiement-portefeuille/etude.md` § 2.2).
5. **A maker's order can come back to the book and be taken again, usually while the app is closed** (§ 1.10): the app may read 6, 7 or 8, then 6, 7 or 8 again, of another contract, without ever seeing the order public in between. A maker seller is then asked a second escrow, a maker buyer a second payout invoice. The phase 2 records pin the first escrow's payment hash for ever and keep one payment per step, and the wallet's payout invoice is one per trade: as they stand, a second contract would be taken for a breach (§ 3.2, § 3.5).
6. **The trade's sats are fixed by the coordinator at the take, at its price then** (§ 1.9): the 2 % check of the taker against the price seen just before the take has no equivalent anchor for a maker, and a maker buyer is exposed from the first APK to receiving fewer sats than the market gives (§ 3.3).
7. **The range comes with the advanced options**: opening them switches the form to « Amount Range » by default (§ 1.11), so a form with advanced options and no range would not be the reference's.

## 3. Architecture

Everything stays inside `features/p2p` and `packages/robosats`, under the rules of the fork's section of `AGENTS.md` (« Troc (fork) »), as in phase 2 (the phase 2 note § 3). Nothing goes into `lib/core`; the shell lends nothing new: the wallet's port of #100 pays the maker bond as it pays a taker bond.

### 3.1 Package (`packages/robosats`)

- **`makeOrder({coordinator, network, identity, request: MakeOrderRequest})`**, `POST /api/make/` on the robot's own session with the short header, returning `Result<MakeOrderOutcome, OrderActionFailure>`. The short header is the phase 2 deviation (the phase 2 note § 3.1): the reference sends the full header with the keys (§ 1.2), while here the robot was put on the coordinator first (`PutRobotOnCoordinatorUsecase`), so the coordinator ignores the keys of a token it knows. `MakeOrderRequest` is a checked value: an order type, a `RobosatsCurrency` (not 1000, § 7, proposal 4), a fixed `FiatAmount` or a range within the coordinator's ratios (1.5 to 15), the payment method text (at most 70 characters, printable, cleaned of control and bidi characters), a premium from −100 (excluded) to 999 with two decimals, a public duration of 600 s to 86,400 s, an escrow duration of 1,800 s to 36,000 s, a bond size of 2 to 15 %, both coordinates or none (six decimals, within their bounds), an optional `OrderPasswordHash` (64 lowercase hexadecimal characters, never the password), an optional description (at most 240 characters, cleaned). The body is JSON as the reference writes it (numbers for `amount`, `premium`, `bond_size`, the durations; `null` for what is absent), with `is_explicit` false and `satoshis` null.
- **The answer**: `OrderMade(MadeOrder)` for a 201 whose body is `ListOrderSerializer`'s with `id` ≥ 1, status 0, `expires_at` after `created_at`, and the type, currency, amount or range, premium, payment method, escrow duration, bond size and coordinates **equal to those sent**; the internal user ids `maker` and `taker` are read as nothing. A 201 that differs is `OrderActionOutcomeUnknown` (an order may exist: the read of § 3.4 tells). The answer proves nothing about who made the order (it has no `is_maker`, § 1.2): only the first `GET /api/order/` by the robot, whose parser checks `is_maker` against the robot's hash id (`coordinator_order.dart:845-853`), does.
- **Refusals**: `OrderActionRefused` (nothing was made) for the empty 400 of the form check, 1036, 1037 (`OrderBookFull`), 1038 and 1039, 1004 to 1010 (`OrderSizeRefused`, `OrderLocationRefused`), and 409 with 1000 to 1003 (`RobotHasActiveOrder`, already typed); `OrderActionOutcomeUnknown` for anything else, a timeout included. A POST is never retried, by the transport or by a use case (the phase 2 note § 3.1).
- **`pauseOrder({identity, lastRead})`**: `{"action": "pause"}`, only from a last read of this robot as the maker at 1 or 2, refused before sending otherwise; the answer is the order's read (`api/views.py:695`), returned as `OrderPauseToggled(CoordinatorOrder)`; 1032 and 1033 are `OrderActionRefused`, the rest unknown. Since the body has no direction, the member says in its documentation that a second send undoes the first, and the use case never sends twice (§ 3.4).
- **`CoordinatorOrder`** already reads a maker's order at 0, 1 and 2 (the statuses, `is_maker`, the bond invoice only for a maker at 0, `coordinator_order.dart:15-23`, `:880-884`, `price_now` and `premium_now`, `:687-693`); the PR adds fixtures of the reference's maker answers at every status and maps error 1022 on a maker's read at 0 to a typed failure (`OrderMakerBondExpired`, the order then read at 5).
- **The money members** become ten: `makeOrder` creates an order whose bond is then asked, `pauseOrder` acts on an order whose bond is locked; both are added to `_moneyMembers` (`features/p2p/test/trade_money_calls_architecture_test.dart:34-45`, `:241-250`) in the PR that adds them, and only the trade use cases name them.
- `fetchLimits` (`GET /api/limits/`) and `fetchInfo` exist (`coordinator_client.dart:43`, `:99`); `fetchKnownRobot` gives `active_order_id` and `last_order_id` (`coordinator_robot.dart:66-69`), which § 3.4 uses.

### 3.2 Domain: the maker's trade and its state machine

- **Origin.** `TradeOrigin.madeHere` beside `takenHere` and `recovered` (`features/p2p/lib/src/domain/trade_record.dart:10-21`), written only by the make use case. A record of origin `madeHere` without an order id is a **pending make** (§ 3.4); `TradeRecord.orderId` stays an `int`: the repository gives a pending make as its own type (`PendingMake`), so that the reviewed users of `TradeRecord` never see a record without an order.
- **What the maker asked: `MakerTerms`.** Set once, before the request leaves, never read again from the coordinator: the network, the coordinator, the order type and the role it gives (**the maker of a buy order buys**, the opposite of `tradeRoleTaking`, `trade_terms.dart:12-15`), the currency, the fixed amount or the range, the premium, the payment method text, the bond size, the public and escrow durations, whether there is a description (and its text, § 3.5), whether there is a password (never the password nor its hash), the coordinates sent, the coordinator's price for the currency read from `/api/limits/` just before the request (`createdPrice`), and the maker fee of `/api/info/` (refused above 1 %, as the taker fee is, `TradeTolerances.takerFeeCeiling`, `trade_tolerances.dart:31`).
- **`TradeTerms` for both sides.** The terms the state machine checks keep one type, so that every use case that compares an answer with `terms.orderId`, `terms.coordinator`, `terms.network` or `terms.role` (the cancel, the chat step, the rating, the reader) is unchanged; what depends on the side moves into a sealed member: `TakenSide` (the amount taken, the book's price, the taker fee: today's fields, `trade_terms.dart:48-66`) or `MadeSide` (the `MakerTerms`, and once the take has fixed it, the amount taken of a range, read from the order). The two alternatives are worse: a separate `MakerTerms`-only state machine would duplicate every guard and the reader; widening today's fields with nullable maker fields would touch the taker's checks, reviewed in phase 2, at every line. Storage follows (§ 3.5): a taker's terms stay in `trade_terms`, a maker's go to a new `maker_terms`.
- **Positions**: three new ones, `makerBondStep` (0), `public` (1) and `paused` (2) (`trade_edges.dart:4-60`, where 0, 1 and 2 are today only refusals, `trade_status.dart:470-474`). A maker is always a participant: it is never at `bondStep`, `preTakeUnavailable`, `publicAgain` or `notAvailable`, and error 1044, which the coordinator gives only to a robot that is neither maker nor taker (`api/views.py:269-275`), is refused for a maker (`TradeStatusUnexpected`).
- **The start**: `TradeStatus.afterMake(first read)`, from a fresh trail. A maker's trail is written only once its order id is known (§ 3.5), so a pending make has none, and `afterMake` starts the trail in three places: after the make's own answer, at the resolution of a make whose answer was lost, and for a confirmed make whose first trail was not written before the app was killed (§ 3.4). It is named only there, as `TradeTrail.forPendingTake` is named only by the take since the review of #117 (`robot_bindings_architecture_test.dart:507-510`; the comment of `trade_status.dart:93-96`, which still names the resolution, is out of date). From `start`, a maker's first read may be `makerBondStep` or `expired` (5, reason 1), each showing this robot as the maker; error 1043 ends the trade as `cancelled` but proves nothing about the maker, since its answer carries no participant's field (`api/views.py:258-260`): it never confirms a make (§ 3.4). No invoice was ever shown before that first read, so nothing can have been paid.

### 3.3 The trade's sats, fixed at the take: what the app checks (the study's « E4 »)

**The question.** For a taker, the sats of the trade are checked within 2 % of the sats of the amount at the book's price read just before the take (`trade_status.dart:571-578`, `TradeTolerances.price`), because the coordinator fixes them seconds or minutes later (`api/logics.py:1332-1335`). For a maker, they are fixed at the take, at the coordinator's price then, up to 24 hours after the creation, often while the app is closed (§ 1.9), and the coordinator checks no size at that moment (sizes are checked at the creation only, `api/views.py:201-205`, `api/logics.py:138-177`). Two different tools are at stake, which phase 2 has both of and which must not be confused: a **warning** (`TradeWarning`), which says the coordinator contradicted the terms, hides the QR code behind « Show anyway » and stops « Pay with this wallet » for the trade; and a **ceiling** of the wallet (`walletPaymentFor`'s `accepted`, `wallet_payment.dart:59-75`, `TradeTerms.escrowCeiling`, `trade_terms.dart:301-310`), which only bounds what this wallet pays and says nothing about the coordinator.

**No warning on the price.** Reusing the taker's warning against the price at the creation would fire after any normal move of the market over a day, and would push a maker seller to pay its escrow blind from another wallet. The alternatives as warnings are no better: a wider tolerance is arbitrary and still fires; a fresh `/api/limits/` read at the first read of 6, 7 or 8 is the same coordinator's price at the moment the app looks, not at the take, and misfires when the app opens hours later; the coordinator's `price_now` last read at 1 or 2 runs only when the app was open around the take, needs a price stored in the trail, and like the previous one protects only against a coordinator that contradicts itself; a third party's price (the wallet's) is an invention, another index, and a new dependency on the shell. So, as the reference: no warning compares `last_satoshis` with a price. What stays checked, from 6 on, for a maker: the order's terms (`TradeTermsChanged`); the escrow invoice's amount equals `escrow_satoshis` equals `trade_satoshis` (`EscrowAmountMismatch`); the payout amount equals `trade_satoshis` (`PayoutAmountMismatch`); the implied fee, `escrow − last_satoshis` for a seller or `last_satoshis − invoice_amount` for a buyer, between 0 and `last_satoshis` × the maker fee of the terms + 1 sat (`TradeTolerances.feeSats`), the maker fee refused above 1 % at the creation.

**Proposed: a wide ceiling for the wallet, and the gap said before every step that commits money** (an open proposal, § 7, proposal 7, whose factor is the owner's to choose):

- **The ceiling.** « Pay with this wallet » pays a maker seller's escrow only up to the sats of the amount taken at the creation's rate (`createdPrice` × (1 + premium)), times a factor F, plus the maker fee of them and 1 sat, rounded down; the review proposes F = 2. Above it, the wallet's block says « The amount asked is very far from the order's price when you made it. Pay it with another wallet if you accept it. » (ours) and only « Pay with another wallet » stays, with the QR code and the copy as usual: it is not a warning, not a breach, and it blocks nothing paid from another wallet.
- **The gap.** Before the escrow's confirmation (maker seller), before « Submit » of the payout and before « Confirm … sent » (maker buyer), one line of ours, information only: « {coordinator} fixed {sats} sats for {amount} {currency} when your order was taken: a rate of {rate} {currency}/BTC, {gap} % from the rate when you made it ({createdRate}). » The buyer's lines come with APK 1, since a maker buyer is exposed from the first order (§ 2.0, fact 6).

**The worst cases, honestly**, a coordinator lying at the take:

- **Maker buyer**: it can fix `last_satoshis` as low as it likes; the payout is that less the fee (`api/logics.py:650-669`), while the buyer still sends the whole fiat amount: up to the whole trade's value lost, as in the reference. No ceiling applies (the buyer pays no sats); the gap line shows it before « Submit » and before « Confirm … sent », and the ways out then cost the bond (no payout in time, `api/logics.py:423-443`) or end in a dispute after the chat.
- **Maker seller, without a ceiling** (this note's first version): the escrow can be any amount, and « Pay with this wallet » would pay it on one confirmation: bounded only by the Instant Payments balance and the swap service's limits, which the code does not give.
- **Maker seller, with the ceiling at F = 2**: at most about twice the trade's value at the creation's rate, plus the fee, per escrow, and with the rule of § 4.3 at most one escrow held through this wallet at a time for the order. The ceiling fires in a normal market only if the bitcoin price halves between the creation and the escrow (at most the 24 hours of the public duration and the escrow step). From another wallet nothing bounds it, as in the reference; the gap line informs.
- In every case each sat locked is exposed: the coordinator holds the preimage and can settle any escrow (`api/AGENTS.md`, « Dispute custody model »). The ceiling bounds the exposure through this wallet, not the coordinator's power over an escrow of honest size.

**Still checked at 0**: the maker bond within 2 % of the bond at the price read just before the make (§ 3.2), a warning as in phase 2, since the coordinator makes it seconds after the creation.

### 3.4 Creating, and a make whose answer was lost

`MakeOrderUsecase`, on the model of `TakeOrderUsecase` (`take_order_usecase.dart:22-32`), each step only once the one before it succeeded:

1. Without a robot: the coordinator is in the federation list on the network; its `/api/info/` (the maker fee, under its ceiling) and `/api/limits/` (the price and the limits of the currency) are read anonymously; the amount is within the form's limits (§ 1.3); the `MakerTerms` are built. A make refused here costs no robot.
2. A fresh robot is reserved and bound to that coordinator (`TradeRobots.robotForNewTrade`), then put on it (`PutRobotOnCoordinatorUsecase`), which already refuses a robot that existed there or held an order (`take_order_usecase.dart:27`); never a pasted key's robot (#79). This is what makes the resolution of step 6 sound: the robot never had another order on that coordinator.
3. The pending make (a `trades` row of origin `madeHere`, no order id, § 3.5) and its `maker_terms` are written, in that order; the make is refused when either fails.
4. `makeOrder` is sent once, through the robot's places in `RobotRequestLimiter`, and never again; one make at a time per network in the process (a lock as `TradeTakeLocks`, keyed by network since no order id exists yet).
5. The answer: `OrderMade(id)` → the robot reads `GET /api/order/?order_id=id`; when the read shows this robot as the maker (§ 3.1), the order id and `confirmed_at` are written together, then the first trail from `TradeStatus.afterMake`, and the bond is shown from that stored status only. A refusal known to come before the action (an empty 400, a 401 or 403 of the authentication, 1037 to 1039, 1004 to 1010, 409 with 1000 to 1003) closes the pending make (« not made »). An outcome unknown leaves it pending.
6. **Resolution of a pending make** (at the next open of the trade, of the list of active trades, or of the app): the robot is read back (`fetchKnownRobot`, short header, through the limiter): its `active_order_id`, else its `last_order_id`, names the only order this robot can have made (step 2); that order is read, and step 5 goes on from there: a read showing this robot as the maker confirms it; error 1043, which proves nothing, closes the pending make as not made (nothing was shown, nothing paid). When the robot shows neither, and its read was sent after the make's request ended (answered or timed out), the pending make is closed as not made: a request still in flight that lands later makes an order waiting at 0 for a bond invoice that this app never shows, which the coordinator expires in 300 s with nothing paid (`api/logics.py:329-338`), and the robot stays bound and is never used again. The reference needs none of this, its robot living in the page that sent the request; here it is the rule of the pending take (the phase 2 note § 4.5) applied to a request whose order id is unknown.

**The app killed between two writes**, the two cases phase 2's rules imply:

- A pending make without its `maker_terms` (killed between the two writes of step 3) never sent its request: it is closed at once, with no request, as phase 2 closes a pending take without its terms or first trail (`list_active_trades_usecase.dart:93-95`, the phase 2 note § 4.5). The database lets an order id be written only for a robot whose `maker_terms` exist (§ 3.5).
- A confirmed make without its first trail (killed between the two writes of step 5) is **not** closed as never sent, which the pending take's rule would do since #117: its order exists. It resumes from `afterMake` at its next read, which can only be 0, 5 or 1043, since no invoice was ever shown.

**The screens of a pending make.** The trade screen's session follows a `TradeRecord` (`trade_watch_session.dart:112`, `:162`, `:361`), which a pending make is not (§ 3.2): the list of active trades shows a pending make as its own line, « Creating your order… », whose screen runs the resolution, and becomes the trade once confirmed.

The other use cases: `PauseOrderUsecase` (reads the order again with the robot, sends `pauseOrder` only when the fresh read's status is the one the user saw, 1 to pause or 2 to resume, never twice, then reads again); `CancelTradeUsecase` widened to the maker's statuses (§ 3.2); the renewal (slice 2) is a new `MakeOrderUsecase` run from the stored `MakerTerms` of the expired order, the password asked again; `PayBondFromWalletUsecase` takes the maker bond at a stored `WaitingForMakerBond` (its `_atStep`, `pay_bond_from_wallet_usecase.dart:278-281`) under the step `bond`, the robot's only bond, and a maker seller's escrow at 6 and 7 under the ceiling of § 3.3 and the rule of § 4.3, recorded under its contract (§ 3.5); `PayoutFromWalletUsecase` keys the wallet's invoice by trade **and contract** (`network:coordinatorAlias:orderId:take`), where it is one per trade today (`payout_from_wallet_usecase.dart:10-11`, `:51-56`), so that a maker buyer's new contract gets a new invoice and the old one, of another amount, is never given again.

### 3.5 Stored on the device: `p2p.sqlite` schema 8

Phase 2's tables are taker-shaped in their CHECKs, which SQLite cannot change in place: `trades` accepts the origins `takenHere` and `recovered` only and needs an order id (`features/p2p/lib/src/data/p2p_tables.dart:136-140`); `trade_terms` enforces the taker's role rule and holds a taker fee (`:203`, `:212`); `trade_trails` lists the taker's positions (`:274`), keeps the deadline of the last read only (`deadline`, `domain/trade/trade_storable.dart:167-168`) and its trigger pins the first escrow hash for ever (`trade_trails.drift:79`); `trade_payments` keeps one row per trade and step (`p2p_tables.dart:317`). Schema 8 is one step for all of it, in its own pull request before any maker code, so that the user's records are migrated once and reviewed once.

Each rebuilt table keeps its own copy until its own rows are back, and no block drops another table's copy. Then `maker_terms` and its triggers, then the replaced trigger of `trade_terms`: drift's `createTrigger` writes `CREATE TRIGGER IF NOT EXISTS` (`p2p_database.steps.dart:273`), which would leave the old one in place, so each replaced trigger is preceded by a constant `DROP TRIGGER IF EXISTS`, which makes the step safe to run again. The order: `trades` first, since the other tables' triggers and foreign keys name it.

**The foreign keys must be off** while the step drops `trades`, the parent of five tables: with them on, `DROP TABLE` runs an implicit delete that the children's rows would make fail, at every opening. They are off by default and turned on only in `beforeOpen`, after any step (`p2p_database.dart:37-40`, `:176`), but no test checks it during a migration today (the migration tests check that they are on afterwards, `features/p2p/test/migrations_test/p2p_database/migration_test.dart:626`, `:969`). So the step reads `PRAGMA foreign_keys` and refuses to drop `trades` when it is on, and runs `PRAGMA foreign_key_check` on the rebuilt tables and their children once every row is back, before the step ends: a violation stops the step, the file stays at schema 7 with its copies, and the feature reports its records unreadable (`P2pStorageFailure`) rather than write a schema 8 file with orphan rows.

The rows keep every value; the new columns take their defaults (`take` 0, `take_deadline` and `taker_hash_id` `NULL`, filled at the next read of a running trade). Every statement is a constant pinned as written by `robot_bindings_architecture_test.dart`, as the schema 7 rebuild's are (`:1182-1220`), and none names a binding: the review of that test is the security review of the migration. drift's `TableMigration` is not used (experimental, needs an `ignore`), as in 6 → 7.

**A new contract, step by step**: at the first take the trail keeps contract 0 (its deadline D0, its taker T0) and pins escrow H1; the wallet pays it (payment row `escrow`, take 0). The buyer gives no payout, the order goes back to the book, H1 is returned at once by the coordinator, and someone takes again, all while the app is closed. The next read shows 6 with a deadline D1 later than D0: in one write, the trail moves to `take` 1, keeps D1 and the new taker, and pins the new escrow H2. The wallet's records show H1's swap failed and its refund pending, so « Pay with this wallet » is offered for H2, after a confirmation that says this is a new contract, under the payment row of take 1; H1's refund goes on under its own row (`BondPaymentLine` of #97). An escrow that changes within one contract, an escrow equal to one already paid, or a taker that changes under the same deadline is a breach, as in phase 2.

**Not stored**, as in phase 2: invoices, the coordinator's answers, the robot's pair, the chat; and, new: **the order's password**, neither in clear nor hashed (only whether there is one); the renewal asks it again, as the reference does.

## 4. Security and privacy

### 4.1 Threat model for a maker

| Who | Can | The app's answer |
|---|---|---|
| The order's coordinator | fix the trade's sats at a false price at the take (§ 3.3) | not detected, as in the reference; the order's terms, the escrow's and the payout's agreement with `trade_satoshis` and the implied fee are checked; the escrow confirmation states the sats, the amount and the rate they imply before this wallet pays |
| | ask a maker bond of the wrong amount | checked within 2 % of the bond at the price read just before the make; outside, a warning, the QR code behind « Show anyway », never paid from this wallet; the wallet pays at most the confirmed amount, within that ceiling |
| | give the invoice of another order or another node | the amount, the network and the expiry checked, the payment hash pinned for the order's life (the bond) or for the take (the escrow); the payee is in practice not verifiable (the phase 2 note § 4.4); the description (« Publishing 'Order {id}: …' - Maker bond », `api/logics.py:1273`) is shown cleaned, not checked |
| | answer the make with someone else's order | the 201 proves nothing; the first read proves `is_maker` against this robot's hash id (§ 3.1) before an invoice is shown |
| | keep the maker bond, settle it unfairly, decide a dispute against the maker | custodian by design, as for a taker; one robot per order limits it to that order |
| | swallow an answer so that the app sends again | a make, a pause and a cancel are each sent once and never again; a lost make is resolved by reading the robot (§ 3.4) |
| A taker | take and never act: the maker bond stays locked and the order goes back to the book, the taker's bond settled and half of it credited to the maker robot (§ 1.10) | the reference's answer; for a maker seller every such round asks a new escrow, so a new swap's fees and a refund that takes time, possibly two escrows out at once: said in the trial script of slice 3 |
| | lie in the chat, not pay, open a dispute | phase 2's warnings and dialogs, unchanged |
| The swap service (when this wallet pays) | hold the maker bond for the order's life: up to about 51 hours with the defaults, 58 at the maximums (§ 1.4), and if the coordinator never releases it, until its CLTV, about 650 to 740 blocks, 4.5 to 5.1 days; hold a maker seller's escrow as a taker seller's (#97); refund late (32 hours the one time observed) | « Pay with another wallet » always offered; these figures in the trial scripts; one maker bond was held by the service once, cancelled before any take (§ 2.0) |
| The app killed between the make and its answer | leave an order this app does not know | the pending make, written before the request and resolved by the robot's read (§ 3.4) |
| Nobody, while the app is closed | the order is taken while the app is closed; the maker's step runs out (3 hours by default) and the maker bond is lost | nothing in code before the end of the project (the owner's decision of 2026-11-16); said in every trial script; the 10-hour escrow timer from slice 2; the question put again at the end of the phase (§ 7, proposal 1) |
| A stolen, unlocked phone | as in the phase 2 note § 4.1; and make orders, pay their bonds from this wallet, take them from elsewhere and cancel as maker at 6 to have half of each bond credited to its own taker robot | the same class of threat as phase 2's, with the same open question of a PIN before « Pay with this wallet » (the phase 2 note § 7, decision 9) |
| A network observer | see Tor traffic | everything over the embedded Tor to `.onion` addresses; nothing published to Nostr by the app for a make (no account-recovery event, § 7, proposal 16); a meeting point is published by the coordinator itself with the order (slice 4) |

### 4.2 Never logged or stored

- Never logged, printed or put in a fixture, as in phase 2 (the phase 2 note § 4.3): the token and its hash, the header, the invoices, the payment hashes, coordinator text; and, new, the make's body: the amount, the premium, the payment methods, the description, the coordinates, the password and its hash.
- Never stored: the order's password, in clear or hashed (only whether there is one).
- Stored, in `maker_terms` only, never logged: what the maker asked (§ 3.5), the description and the coordinates included, which the coordinator holds and shows anyway; they are the user's own order, kept for the renewal and the terms' checks.

### 4.3 The rule for showing or paying an invoice

The phase 2 rule (the phase 2 note § 4.4) applies unchanged, with two cases more:

- **The maker bond at 0**: shown and offered to this wallet only from a stored status of a confirmed make (its order id read and this robot shown as its maker); its amount equal to `bond_satoshis` and within 2 % of the bond at the creation's price (§ 3.2); its payment hash pinned for the whole life of the order; payable until the earlier of its expiry and the order's deadline (creation + 300 s), less `TradeTolerances.walletPaymentMargin`; paid within what the user confirmed, under the step `bond`.
- **A maker seller's escrow**: pinned and recorded per take (§ 3.5): a new escrow is shown and paid only after the trail has moved through `public` with one more take; « Pay with this wallet » pays within what the user confirms in the escrow confirmation, which states the sats, the amount and the rate they imply (§ 3.3); an escrow that changes within one take is a breach, as in phase 2.

### 4.4 What the independent security reviewer checks in each pull request

- Package: every field of the make bounded before it is sent and the 201 checked against it; no coordinator text in failures; a POST never retried; the password never sent, only its hash, and never logged; `pauseOrder` refused before sending unless the last read shows this robot as the maker at 1 or 2.
- Schema 8: every rebuilt table keeps every row and value (tests with rows of each table, a step stopped after each statement and run again); the statements pinned and naming no binding; the triggers: an order id set once and only with `confirmed_at`, `takes` monotonic and moving by one, an escrow hash released only with it, a bond hash never released, a payment's take never changed; no update or delete of the terms.
- Domain: the maker's edges and only them, the start from a make only, 17 and 18 by side, the bond check and its tolerance, no automatic action on a surprising answer, the robot never reused, bound before its first contact.
- Use cases: the make written before it is sent and sent once; the order id written only from a read that shows this robot as the maker; a pending make closed only on a refusal known to come first or on a robot read sent after the request ended; the pause sent once, after a read that shows the status the user saw; the money members named only in the trade use cases.
- Wallet: the maker bond paid only from a stored `WaitingForMakerBond` and the confirmation's amount; the second escrow under its own record; nothing paid twice.
- UI: what the QR code encodes and what « Pay with this wallet » pays is what was decoded, checked and shown; the escrow confirmation's figures are the status's own; no log.

Each such pull request carries the label `money` and gets two reviews, functional and security, by independent agents with no prior context, as in phase 2.

## 7. Open decisions

Proposals 1 to 10 are put to the owner in plain words, with the default this note proposes; 11 to 18 are technical, with the recommendation first. None of them is decided by this note.

**For the owner:**

1. **Being taken while the app is closed.** Example: you publish a buy order in the evening; someone takes it at 2 a.m.; with the default settings you have until 5 a.m. to give your invoice, otherwise you lose your bond. The official app rings; this one does not, since the background service and the notifications are kept for the end (your decision of 2026-11-16). Default: keep that decision; say the risk in this note and in every trial script; for the trials, be the taker yourself with the official app, or keep the app open, or choose the 10-hour timer once slice 2 offers it; ask the question again before phase 3 is called done.
2. **One new robot for every order, the renewal included.** Example: your order expires untaken; « Renew Order » makes it again, under another robot name and picture, and asks the bond again. The official app keeps the same robot when it can. Default: a new robot each time, since the app already never uses a robot twice and RoboSats itself advises one robot per trade; « Renew » then also works after a trade, where the official app asks you to switch robot.
3. **The order password.** Example: you want to sell to a friend; you set a password, the order stays out of the book, and you send your friend the link and the password; your friend uses the official app, since this one cannot take a protected order yet. Default: offer it as RoboSats does, in the last slice; taking a protected order with this app stays for later.
4. **The « Swap? » mode** (Lightning sats against on-chain bitcoin instead of a currency). Default: not in phase 3, said as a deviation; the wallet already swaps.
5. **« Pay with this wallet » for the maker bond from the first slice.** Example: for an order of 50,000 sats, a bond of 1,500 sats goes to the swap service and can stay there about two days if the order is taken, plus the refund's delay (32 hours once). 6. **The sentence « This box will ring 🔊 once a robot takes your order »**, which this app cannot keep. Default: the reference's sentence word for word, as for the hold-invoice sentence (#135), the limit said in the trial scripts; the other way is a line of ours under it, « Bull Market does not ring yet: open it to see whether your order was taken. ».
7. **Checking the amount fixed at the take.** The sats of your trade are fixed by the coordinator when someone takes your order, at its price at that moment, maybe a day after you made it. Default: as RoboSats, the app does not compare that price with anything (the market moves, and a false alarm would stop « Pay with this wallet »); before you pay a sell order's collateral, the confirmation shows the sats, the amount and the rate they make, so you see a price far from the market; everything else is checked as for a taker (§ 3.3).
